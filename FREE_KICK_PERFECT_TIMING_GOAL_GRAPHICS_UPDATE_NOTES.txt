FREE KICK PERFECT TIMING + GOAL GRAPHICS UPDATE

Gameplay / timing
- Free Kick Training now has a true PERFECT green-zone timing mechanic.
- The PERFECT window scales with the player's Free Kick Accuracy:
  * 95+ gets the widest elite window.
  * 90+ gets a strong elite window.
  * Lower ratings get progressively smaller windows.
- Technique difficulty affects window size: Knuckleball is narrower, Normal is slightly more forgiving.
- For players with 90+ Free Kick Accuracy:
  * Releasing inside the green PERFECT zone guarantees a goal, while still using the selected shot animation and live trajectory.
  * Curl still routes outside the wall before bending back towards goal.
  * Dip, Knuckleball, Normal and Wall-only Low Driven retain their own flight behaviour.
  * Releasing outside the green zone cannot score. Near misses resolve as goalkeeper saves or woodwork; larger timing errors go wide or over.
- The goalkeeper still animates on perfect goals, but the strike is placed beyond the keeper rather than freezing the goalkeeper.
- Timing feedback now identifies PERFECT CONTACT, EARLY RELEASE, LATE RELEASE, GREAT SAVE and OFF THE POST outcomes.

Power-bar GUI
- The green timing area is brighter, labelled PERFECT and scales with player rating/technique.
- Added a visible moving power marker.
- The percentage turns into PERFECT while the marker is inside the green zone.
- The power/timing HUD is moved to the lower-left to keep the player, ball and goal centre unobstructed.
- Technique buttons are slimmer to reduce pitch obstruction.

Live-session GUI
- The duplicate footer stats, Start and Simulate controls disappear once the free-kick session is live.
- Score, time, stars and objective remain available in the compact pitch HUD.
- The pitch expands vertically during the live session.
- Wind display is smaller and less visually dominant.

Goal graphics
- Free-kick goal is larger and easier to read.
- Thicker bright posts and crossbar with no unrealistic bottom frame bar.
- Added visible goal-line cue.
- Added rear support poles, depth shadow and deeper goal structure.
- Cleaner square mesh with larger, more readable spacing.
- Improved roof net, side net and floor net perspective.
- Deeper back-net geometry and more visible net depth.
- Target graphics are smaller/less opaque so they do not hide the frame.

Net physics
- Local impact cell is larger and scales with the enlarged goal.
- Top, side and lower-net impacts retain separate reactions.
- Curl, Dip, Knuckleball, Normal and Low Driven retain technique-specific net response strength.
- Perfect goals still travel into the goal and trigger local net deformation rather than stopping at the line.

Goalkeeper presentation
- Free-kick goalkeeper is positioned slightly deeper and rendered slightly smaller so the goal opening remains readable.
