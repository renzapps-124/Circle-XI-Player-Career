Circle XI Player Career – Free Kick Training Refinement

Changes in this build:
- Corrected training goalkeeper orientation. The keeper is upright when set and remains correctly oriented during dive/catch/parry animations, then returns to the ready pose.
- Reduced the visual size and collision radius of the artificial free-kick mannequins by roughly 30%, creating more realistic scoring lanes.
- Wall Simulation now makes the four-player wall jump after the strike.
- A jumping wall creates a genuine low clearance gap, so low-driven free kicks can pass underneath the wall.
- Added LOW DRIVEN as a selectable free-kick technique in the training HUD.
- Low-driven free kicks use a fast, low trajectory with strong topspin.
- Free Kick Accuracy 95+ now activates elite technique assistance: much tighter aim variation, more forgiving power and viable trajectories for Curl, Top-Spin Dip, Knuckleball, Low Driven and Normal free kicks.
- Removed Random Match Situations from Free Kick Training.
- Removed Free Practice from Free Kick Training.
- Free Kick Training now contains four main sessions: Free Kick Technique, Fixed Target Free Kicks, Wall Simulation and Pressure 10.
