CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V75.13
SAVED GOAL REPLAYS COULD NEVER BE STORED

The diagnosis
The replay system itself was complete and correct. Goals raise a replay prompt,
the buffer is captured, the Save button is wired to saveActiveReplay, the gallery
renders cards with thumbnails, and the player draws real footballers with kits,
animations, ball height and pitch graphics. None of that was broken.

The clip simply could not be written to disk. serialiseReplayFrame stored every
field of every player as a named JSON key at full float precision — plus an 80
node net mesh — for every frame:

  one serialised frame        9,667 bytes
  frames kept per clip              300
  one clip                      4.39 MB

A career is written to localStorage twice, once under the live key and again
inside the career slot, so a single clip needed roughly 9 MB against a 5 MB
budget. Every save threw QuotaExceededError. persistCareerSlots caught it,
trimmed backups (which are tiny and did not help), logged a console warning, and
returned. The clip — and the rest of that save — was silently discarded. Zero
clips could ever fit, which is why the gallery never showed a goal.

The fix
1. COMPACT CLIP FORMAT (version 4)
   - The net mesh is not stored. It is decoration and the player redraws it.
   - Player ids are stored once per clip instead of on every player of every
     frame, and the per-frame rows are flat integer arrays with no key names.
   - Repeated strings (action, variant, foot, dive height) go through a per-clip
     dictionary and are stored as indices.
   - Numbers are quantised: positions and velocities to a tenth, angles to a
     milliradian, timers to a hundredth.
   - The saved window is 8 seconds around the goal rather than 26.

     clip                          size      fits
     old clip (300 frames)         4496 KB      NO
     new clip (80 frames)            98 KB      yes
     new, 10 clips                  978 KB      yes

   46x smaller. Ten clips now sit comfortably inside the quota.

   The quantisation is not visible. Over 80 frames of 22 players:

     frames                80/80
     id mismatches         0
     action mismatches     0
     max position error    0.0500 px
     max angle error       0.00050 rad

2. DECODED ON DEMAND
   Clips are stored compact and decoded once, lazily, when something actually
   draws them, with the result cached on the clip. Frame counts (used by the
   corrupt check on every career load, and by the gallery) read the row count
   directly and never trigger a decode. The playback renderer is unchanged — it
   still receives exactly the frame objects it always expected.

3. STORAGE CAN NO LONGER FAIL SILENTLY
   - A 1.2 MB budget across all saved clips, with the oldest evicted first and an
     inbox message when that happens.
   - If localStorage still refuses the write, saved replays are now what gets
     dropped — they are by far the largest thing in a career — and the player is
     told, rather than the whole save being lost without a word.

The decoder still accepts a clip that carries a raw frames array in case one is
held in memory.
