CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TECHNICAL ATTRIBUTE MATCH GAMEPLAY V39

Purpose
Make the Technical attributes selected in Player Creation and shown in the Profile directly affect how the user's footballer behaves during a match. User input remains important: attributes modify execution, consistency, animation timing and assistance rather than playing the match for the user.

IMPLEMENTED MATCH EFFECTS

1. FIRST TOUCH
- First Touch is now the primary reception/control attribute.
- Higher First Touch reduces loose-control probability on fast, bouncing and pressured receptions.
- Higher First Touch shortens the control/settle animation.
- Holding a movement direction as the ball arrives can produce a directional first touch into space.
- Better First Touch allows a larger, cleaner escape touch without losing control.

2. DRIBBLING
- Dribbling now affects on-ball speed retention.
- Higher Dribbling produces sharper changes of direction while carrying the ball.
- Higher Dribbling reduces heavy-touch risk while sprinting, turning or under pressure.
- The ball is kept physically closer to high-rated dribblers during normal and sprint dribbling.

3. PASSING
- Passing is now the dominant execution rating for normal ground passes.
- It has a larger effect on directional error and pass-weight consistency.
- Technique and First Touch support execution, while mental attributes still affect decision/assistance quality.
- Low Passing remains usable with good player input but is visibly less consistent.

4. TECHNIQUE
- Technique now directly changes preparation speed for difficult football actions.
- High Technique reduces wind-up time for first-time passes/shots, crosses, lofted passes, backheels, finesse shots, chips, volleys, bicycle kicks, free kicks and corners.
- Existing contextual advanced kick animations continue to use Technique gates.

5. CROSSING
- Crossing is now the dominant rating in open-play cross execution.
- Higher Crossing tightens the delivery error cone and makes target zones more repeatable.
- Crossing continues to work with Technique, Passing and the player's selected power/aim.

6. FINISHING
- Finishing has the strongest effect on shots close to goal.
- Higher Finishing reduces directional error and improves clean execution in the box.
- Finishing no longer acts like a raw leg-power stat; it primarily improves execution quality.

7. LONG SHOTS
- Finishing and Long Shots now blend smoothly with distance rather than switching at a hard distance threshold.
- Close shots rely mainly on Finishing.
- As distance increases, Long Shots progressively becomes the dominant shooting attribute.
- Long Shots also affects long-range shot execution consistency for AI players.

8. TACKLING
- Tackling now affects physical tackle reach and clean-contact timing as well as the success/foul calculation.
- Better Tackling gives a slightly larger legal contact window.
- Better Tackling reduces user tackle recovery/cooldown time.
- Slide length/reach also reflects Tackling together with stamina and approach speed.

9. MARKING
- Marking now provides subtle user-controlled defensive assistance.
- When the user holds SPACE to jockey without giving a movement direction, the player shades goal-side of the current opposition ball carrier.
- Higher Marking chooses a tighter contain distance and reacts sooner.
- The system never overrides active WASD movement.

10. PENALTIES
- Penalties is now the dominant penalty-set-piece rating.
- Higher Penalties provides a wider perfect timing area, slower timing needle and lower aiming uncertainty.
- Finishing, Composure, Technique and weak foot remain supporting factors.

11. FREE KICKS
- Free Kicks is now the dominant direct-free-kick rating.
- Higher Free Kicks widens the clean timing window, slows the timing needle and reduces delivery uncertainty.
- Technique, curve, composure and weak foot remain supporting factors.

12. CORNERS
- Corners is now the main match corner-delivery attribute.
- Higher Corners improves the set-piece timing window and reduces target uncertainty.
- Actual corner-flight quality now weights Corners much more heavily, with Crossing and Technique supporting it.

13. THROW INS
- Throw Ins now affects maximum range, aiming uncertainty and actual launch consistency.
- Low ratings introduce more realistic direction and weight variation.
- High ratings reproduce the user's chosen target more faithfully and allow longer throws.
- Technique and Strength provide small supporting contributions.

GENERAL MATCH PRINCIPLE
Attributes modify how faithfully the footballer executes the user's command. Pressure, stamina, weak foot, body angle, movement speed and injury can still reduce effective quality, so a high Technical rating does not create guaranteed outcomes.
