Circle XI Player Career — Training Realism Update

Implemented in the actual game build:
- Walkable Training Ground now uses the same visible footballer renderer used in matches, preserving career face, hair, body/build and boots while applying training clothing.
- Added realistic first-team training wear for the walkable training environment.
- Reworked Training Ground movement to smooth WASD movement with Shift jogging and Enter interaction.
- Improved training dribbling visuals so the controlled ball stays close to the player’s visible boot line with alternating touches rather than floating ahead.
- Upgraded cones, training mannequins, mini goals and full-size goals.
- Full-size training goals now use a sharper square-mesh net design consistent with the match goal appearance.
- Sharpened training pitch presentation with mowing stripes, stronger markings, shadows and more detailed training-ground scenery.
- Added a clubhouse, benches, equipment stations, water rack and clearer training-zone presentation.
- Strengthened Training Ground activity icons/cards for easier navigation.
- Simplified the Training Centre Today UI with clearer hierarchy, compact status cards and direct Start Session / Explore Ground / All Activities actions.
- Existing fatigue, sharpness, intensity, difficulty, progression and training-session systems remain connected.
