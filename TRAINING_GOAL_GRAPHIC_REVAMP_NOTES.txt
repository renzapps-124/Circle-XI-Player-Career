TRAINING GOAL GRAPHIC REVAMP

Applied the new front-facing 2D realistic goal style to training mode, especially Free Kick and target-based training screens.

Changes:
- Rebuilt the goal so it no longer appears visually backwards.
- Front frame is now clearer with a realistic thick white post/crossbar look.
- Roof net, side nets and floor net now recede backwards naturally from the front frame.
- Rear support poles are placed behind the front frame to preserve the correct depth direction.
- Mesh spacing is cleaner and more even for a more realistic square net.
- Fixed target hoops and hanging targets are repositioned so they read as attached to the goal.
- Maintains the existing animated local net impact behaviour.
