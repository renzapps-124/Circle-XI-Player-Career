CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER V49.3
DRIBBLING AND FIRST-TOUCH ANIMATION UPGRADE

DRIBBLING
- Contact-driven ball carrying now distinguishes close control, normal carrying,
  sprint touches, sharp cuts and shielding.
- The active foot alternates with the player's gait and the rendered ball eases
  between contact points instead of snapping to a new boot.
- High Dribbling and First Touch attributes produce tighter, shorter contacts;
  sprinting creates a longer push without changing the existing ball physics.
- Pressure, turn rate and movement speed select the visual touch style in real time.

FIRST TOUCH
- Ground receptions use a withdrawing inside-foot cushion.
- Pressure can trigger sole traps or outside-foot turns.
- Running receptions retain momentum and use a longer reaching step.
- Aerial receptions now have distinct instep, thigh and chest-control poses.
- Chest and thigh controls keep the ball visibly above the grass, then ease it
  down through the contact animation without teleporting the gameplay ball.
- Preferred foot, incoming direction, technique, composure, pressure and pitch
  condition continue to influence the selected reception and its quality.

PRESERVATION
- Passing, shooting, defending, AI decisions, possession, fatigue, career saves,
  pre-match presentation and the existing match physics remain intact.
- The update is visual/contact driven and does not replace the match engine.
