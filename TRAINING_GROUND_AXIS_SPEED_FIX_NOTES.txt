CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER - TRAINING GROUND AXIS SPEED FIX

- Fixed walkable Training Ground movement feeling much faster left/right than up/down.
- Training Ground coordinates are percentage based, so horizontal percentages represented more physical screen pixels on the wide training pitch.
- Horizontal movement is now compensated using the live Training Ground aspect ratio.
- Left/right and up/down movement now cover approximately the same on-screen distance for the same input duration.
- Diagonal movement keeps the existing normalisation, Pace/Acceleration scaling and Shift sprint behaviour.
- The correction adapts automatically to different browser/window sizes instead of using one fixed horizontal speed reduction.
