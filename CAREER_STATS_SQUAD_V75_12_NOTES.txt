CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V75.12
CAREER STATISTICS ACCURACY + INTERNATIONAL SQUAD VIEW

=========================================================================
PART 1 — CAREER STATISTICS WERE COUNTING THE SAME MATCH TWO WAYS
=========================================================================

The game keeps two records of the same fixture:
  A) career.stats, a running counter incremented by accumulateCareerResultStats
  B) fixtureHistory, a full per-match record the season tables are summed from

The Form tab showed A as the headline totals and B as the season breakdown
underneath, so the totals were never guaranteed to add up to the rows they were
supposed to summarise. Feeding one real performance through both paths:

  stat                running counter   season table
  clean sheets                      0              0
  minutes                          90              0
  goals                             1              1
  apps                              1              1

1. CLEAN SHEETS WERE UNREACHABLE FOR OUTFIELD PLAYERS
   Both paths defined a clean sheet as (saves > 0 && conceded === 0). Since an
   outfield player records no saves, a centre-back could play a full season of
   shut-outs and finish on zero. Even a goalkeeper in a 0-0 with no shots faced
   got nothing. Meanwhile the AI squad-mate generator hands defenders clean
   sheets, and the contract system pays a cleanSheetBonus scaled for GK/CB/LB/RB
   — so the rest of the game already assumed defenders earn them.
   The rule is now: the player appeared, and the team conceded nothing.

2. THE SAME FIXTURE WAS WORTH 90 MINUTES OR 0
   The counter defaulted a missing minute count to 90; the match record stored
   0. Both now use the same fallback (90 started, 25 from the bench), so the
   totals card and the season table cannot disagree.

3. THE TWO RECORDS COULD DRIFT APART PERMANENTLY
   Nothing reconciled them. fixtureHistory is the complete per-match record and
   is never trimmed, so it is now the single source of truth: the stored totals
   are rebuilt from it, and the Form tab reads the derived values rather than a
   merge of both. Verified by deliberately corrupting the stored totals:

     total                       stored     after     expected
     Club appearances                99        12           12
     Club goals                      42         3            3
     Club minutes                 12345      1080         1080
     International caps              77         3            3

   Confirmed in the running game: with those corrupt values seeded, the Form tab
   reported 12 appearances / 3 goals / 1,080 minutes and 4 clean sheets, the
   season row agreed exactly, and the Player ID card — which reads career.stats
   directly — also showed 12 / 3 / 3.

4. INTERNATIONAL CAPS COULD ONLY EVER GO UP
   ensureInternationalStatFields used Math.max against the stored value, so any
   figure that was ever too high could never come back down. Caps, goals and
   assists are now taken from the match record.

5. OLD SAVES ARE REPAIRED
   normaliseCareerStatHistory recomputes minutes and clean sheets on every load
   from data already in the row, so existing careers get corrected totals rather
   than only new matches being counted properly.

=========================================================================
PART 2 — INTERNATIONAL SQUAD VIEW
=========================================================================

Career tab -> View Squad now opens with a club / country switch at the top of
the list, and the international squad uses exactly the same table, filters,
sorting, search, player detail panel and two-player comparison as the club one.

  Club squad            18 players
  International squad   23 players

- The national pool is every player registered to a club in the player's own
  country. loadCircleXIManagerSquad generates and caches the whole country in
  one pass, so walking every club is a map lookup after the first call.
- Selection uses a real positional quota (3 GK, 8 DEF, 7 MID, 5 ATT) taking the
  best available in each group, with one place freed in the player's own group
  so they are always in their own national squad.
- Caps, international goals, assists and cards for the player are their real
  international record, not the club one. Squad-mates get caps seeded from age
  and ability. Squad role reads Uncapped / Capped / Called up for the player.
- Countries without a generated database (fallback nationalities) previously
  produced a one-man squad; they now field a full deterministic call-up.
- The heading and eyebrow switch between the club name and the country, and
  switching views resets the selection, comparison, filter and search so state
  cannot leak between the two squads.
