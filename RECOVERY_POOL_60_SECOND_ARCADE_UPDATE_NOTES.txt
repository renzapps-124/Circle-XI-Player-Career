Circle XI Player Career - Recovery Pool 60 Second Arcade Update
13 August 2026

Implemented changes

1. Same career player renderer in Recovery Pool
- The controlled swimmer now uses drawVisibleFootballer(), the same player renderer used during matches.
- Carries across the career player's saved skin tone, face/head rendering, hairstyle, hair colour, body build and proportions.
- Pool appearance switches the match model to a topless swimming presentation with swim shorts and bare feet rather than creating a separate substitute player identity.
- Added a swimming pose mode to the shared footballer renderer so the same model can be reused without changing match presentation.

2. Fixed one minute session
- All playable swimming sessions now run for exactly 60 seconds.
- HUD starts at 01:00.0 and counts down to zero.
- The user completes as many 25m lengths as possible before TIME.
- Wall turns and length split timing remain active during the one minute session.

3. Energy recovery
- Energy still drains through repeated hard strokes.
- Reducing stroke frequency starts light energy recovery.
- Stopping long enough starts faster recovery.
- Stamina directly controls how quickly the player regenerates energy.
- Stamina also continues to reduce stroke cost and passive energy drain.
- Very low energy reduces sustainable swim speed rather than freezing the player.
- HUD displays RECOVERING feedback and changes the energy bar to a bright recovery state.

4. Career attributes
- Pace/Sprint Speed controls maximum swimming speed.
- Acceleration influences how quickly swim speed builds.
- Stamina controls stroke cost, passive drain, easy recovery, rest recovery and turn recovery.
- Simulated swimming now also represents a one minute session using these attributes.

5. Arcade UI update
- Brighter cyan pool presentation.
- Purple, pink, orange and yellow arcade HUD.
- Colourful TIME LEFT, recovery, stroke and energy cards.
- Chunkier J/L buttons and stronger interaction feedback.
- GOOD, GREAT and PERFECT stroke callouts.
- Green animated energy recovery state.
- Colourful arcade results screen and length split board.
- Results show distance, full lengths, best length, rhythm, energy and fatigue recovery.

6. Recovery design
- Recovery Swim rewards controlled pacing, rhythm and finishing with energy remaining.
- One minute duration keeps the activity short enough to use regularly in Career Mode.
- Swimming remains a recovery activity and removes fatigue rather than creating normal training load.

Validation
- game.js passes Node JavaScript syntax validation.
- Final ZIP passes archive integrity validation.
