Circle XI Player Career - Performance Gym Treadmill Player Model V10

Implemented player-model and running-animation replacement for Treadmill Intervals.

PLAYER MODEL
- Keeps the saved shared career-player identity used by Match Day / swimming training.
- Uses fixed upper-arm, forearm, thigh and shin lengths; no treadmill limb stretching.
- Uses a real pelvis / hip anchor under visible gym shorts.
- Shorts are rendered over both upper thighs so the legs visibly emerge from the garment.
- Side-view shoulder depth replaces the old full-width shoulder separation that made arms appear detached.
- Athletic limb mass increased: thicker thighs, calves, upper arms and forearms with tapered silhouettes.
- Trainers are enlarged slightly and articulate from the ankle.
- Side-on torso is slightly compressed in depth while retaining shared player body proportions.

RUNNING ANIMATION
- Rebuilt treadmill gait around stance and swing phases instead of circular leg motion.
- Stance: foot plants ahead of the body and travels backwards with the belt.
- Swing: rear foot lifts through recovery and prepares for the next strike.
- Stance duration shortens at sprint speed to create a genuine flight phase.
- Phase-aware knee pole guides allow a natural trailing leg without the old backwards-knee flip.
- Minimum leg extension varies by speed to stop extreme folded puppet poses.
- Arms use fixed-length two-bone IK with proper running reach rather than collapsing near the shoulder.
- Opposite arm/leg counter-swing, body bounce, landing compression, torso counter-rotation, breathing and fatigue remain active.
- Far-side limbs are darker and slightly offset for clearer depth separation.
- Foot contact shadows make stance and flight easier to read.

TREADMILL FIT
- Machine deck and console reduced slightly so the player is the focal point.
- Working speed and target display remain intact.
- J/L interval gameplay, Pace/Acceleration/Stamina interaction and A+ maximum grade remain unchanged.

VALIDATION
- JavaScript syntax checked with node --check game.js.
- Browser demo loaded without page errors.
- Shared player identity audit returned true.
- Treadmill renderer audit reports fixedBones=true, sharedMatchModel=true,
  properPelvis=true, shortsOverThighs=true and zero leg/arm stretch in tested frames.
