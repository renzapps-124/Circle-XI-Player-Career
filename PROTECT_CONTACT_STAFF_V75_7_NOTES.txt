CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V75.7
PROTECT-THE-BALL CONTACT ANIMATION + CAREER SUPPORT STAFF

=========================================================================
PART 1 — SHIELDING AND BEING MUSCLED OFF THE BALL
=========================================================================

Starting point
The match renderer already had an isShield and an isStumble branch, but they
were thin (a small torso lean and one arm offset each) and — more importantly —
the Protect the Ball drill never triggered either of them. setTrainingVisualAction
had no 'shield' or 'stumble' entry in its action map, so even a direct call would
have fallen through to the 'pass' pose. Being muscled off the ball was a line of
text: technicalScore(s,0,'MUSCLED OFF THE BALL',...) with nothing else happening.
The player kept their run cycle through the whole duel and the ball stayed glued
to their boot after losing it.

1. SHIELD IS NOW A STANCE THE PLAYER HOLDS
   - Engages on the action blend rather than a one-shot sine, so it can be held
     for as long as SPACE is held without the pose pumping in and out.
   - Wide braced leg base with the weight sunk through bent knees (the legs were
     previously untouched by the shield branch).
   - Near arm comes up across the body to hold the defender off; the far arm
     drops low over the ball.
   - Torso leans into the contact, head turns towards the defender.
   - A live brace tremor driven by idleAnim keeps the stance from looking frozen.
   - actionVariant carries which side the contact is on, worked out in body space
     from the cross product of the player's facing and the defender's bearing, so
     it stays correct whatever way the player is turned.
   - The ball is now tucked to the far side of the boots, away from the defender,
     which is what the drill has always asked the player to do.
   - The marker jockeys throughout the duel instead of standing in a run cycle.

2. BEING MUSCLED OFF IS NOW A REAL COLLISION
   - One-shot shove that peaks then recovers, rather than a symmetric bob.
   - Leading foot is knocked across the body and the trailing leg drags behind.
   - Both arms fly out to catch balance, the leading arm higher than the trailing.
   - Head and torso are thrown away from the contact.
   - The user is physically pushed off the contact point and their momentum is
     killed.
   - The ball comes loose and runs towards the defender instead of staying stuck
     to the boot; it keeps rolling through the reset window.
   - The marker plays a shoulder-barge at the same moment.
   - The stage shakes briefly on impact.
   - The reset window went 700ms -> 1050ms so the animation can finish before the
     next scenario spawns.

3. PLUMBING
   - setTrainingVisualAction(action, variant, length) now takes an explicit
     variant and duration; the old single-argument calls are unaffected.
   - 'shield', 'stumble' and 'shoulder' added to the action map and durations.

=========================================================================
PART 2 — CAREER SUPPORT STAFF (from the screenshot)
=========================================================================

4. EVERY STAFF NAME WAS INVISIBLE  (the defect in the screenshot)
   The finance centre paints light cards (rgba(255,255,255,.9)) inside the dark
   app shell. Its group label, benefit line and weekly cost all set their own
   colour, but the staff name <b> did not, so it inherited body{color:#cbd5e1}
   and rendered light grey on near-white — legible only as a smudge. The ledger
   labels had the same problem.
   Fix: the finance centre now sets its own ink, with explicit colours for the
   staff name, the hired state and the ledger labels.

5. THE FILTER ROW LIED ABOUT ITS STATE
   The first group button was rendered with class="active" while no filtering
   had been applied, so "Development" looked selected next to a full unfiltered
   grid. All is now the default active pill, which matches what is shown.

6. STAFF COULD NEVER BE RELEASED
   Hiring was permanent: once hired you paid the weekly cost for the rest of the
   career with no way out. Owned cards now carry a Release action that removes
   the staff member and the weekly cost, logs it to the ledger and posts an inbox
   message.

7. THE ELITE AGENT DID NOTHING AT ALL
   A GBP 9,000 purchase with a GBP 650 weekly cost — the most expensive hire in
   the game and the largest recurring drain — had no effect anywhere in the code.
   It now feeds suggestedContractOffer() and submitContractCounter():

     term                 without agent   with agent
     Weekly wage                  6,200        6,700
     Signing bonus               21,100       24,900
     Appearance bonus               325          350
     Goal bonus                     425          475
     Contract weeks                 156          182

   (evaluation 71, current wage 4,200) plus a one-step lift to the offered squad
   role, and roughly a quarter less club resistance to wage and role counters.

8. TWO MORE BENEFITS WERE ONLY WRITTEN ON THE CARD
   - Sports Psychologist promised to "protect morale" but touched only attribute
     development. Negative post-match morale swings are now cut to 62%.
   - Performance Nutritionist promised to "improve recovery" but only reduced
     injury chance. Nutritionist (+18%) and Recovery Specialist (+12%) now scale
     both the weekly recovery focus and the recovery-pool drill.
   Every one of the eight hires is now wired to a gameplay hook, and the
   validation fails if any id is left without one.

9. THE STAFF NOW SHOW UP WHERE YOU TRAIN
   A support-staff strip sits at the top of the Training Centre listing who is
   hired, what each one does, the weekly bill, and the live effect totals
   (technical / finishing / physical / mental training rates, injury risk,
   recovery rate, contract terms, opposition detail). It links straight back to
   the hire screen. With nobody hired it explains what the staff would do.
   This joins the analyst's existing hook in the fixture panel, so hires are now
   visible in the Training Centre, the fixture preview, contract talks, weekly
   training, recovery and injury rolls.
