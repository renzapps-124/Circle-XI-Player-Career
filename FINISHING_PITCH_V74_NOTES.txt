CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — FINISHING PITCH V74

Scope
Rebuilt the six Finishing Pitch drills as distinct live football scenarios rather than generic target-shooting variants.

1. TARGET FINISHING
- Feeder plays into the user from changing angles and box positions.
- Goal zones illuminate in low near/far, top corners, high centre and low centre areas.
- The target shrinks and moves as the attempt develops; Composure slows the shrink rate.
- The requested finish rotates between normal, finesse, low driven and chip techniques.
- Scoring weighs Finishing, Technique, Composure, power fit, placement and technique selection.
- Missing the scoring zone or using the wrong technique breaks the streak.

2. 1V1 FINISHING
- Feeder releases the user into a true one-on-one.
- Goalkeeper advances from the line, narrows the angle and tracks the attacker.
- A recovering defender closes from behind.
- The user can carry the ball with normal movement, alter the angle and choose normal, finesse, driven or chip finishes.
- Delaying too long lets the keeper smother the situation.
- Quality uses Finishing, Composure, Dribbling and Technique.

3. PRESSURE FINISHING
- User begins off the ball and must attack the service.
- One defender closes on Standard/Beginner; higher pressure is used at harder settings.
- A very short post-reception shooting window forces quick decisions.
- Defenders physically close the user and can end the attempt if they reach tackling range.
- Quality uses Finishing, Composure, Off Ball and Technique, with an extra pressure penalty for nearby markers.

4. FIRST TIME FINISHING
- Service arrives from varied cut-back/cross/through-ball style angles.
- A settling touch is not allowed.
- The user must strike while the ball is inside the live first-time contact window.
- L gives a normal first-time finish; normal shot combinations still provide placed, driven and chipped contacts.
- Quality uses Finishing, Anticipation and Technique plus timing at the moment of contact.

5. LONG RANGE SHOOTING
- User starts 20–30+ yard equivalents from goal.
- A defender closes the shooting lane after reception.
- L power, J+L placed/curl, K+L driven, I+L chip and J+K+L knuckle are available.
- Quality uses Long Shots, Technique and Composure.
- Successful tighter placement and correct technique score more highly.

6. AERIAL FINISHING
- Whipped/lofted aerial service is played into the box.
- User must attack the delivery inside the live contact window.
- J = Power Header
- K = Glancing Header
- L = Directed Header
- Higher difficulties add stronger aerial defensive pressure.
- Quality uses Heading, Anticipation, Finishing and Technique.

Shared upgrades
- All six drills use a detailed full-size goal, live goalkeeper, shared match player renderer and career appearance/kit identity.
- Goalkeeper save chance uses Reflexes, Positioning and Handling, plus placement and shot quality.
- Goalkeeper uses directional high/mid/low dive variants.
- Goal net animation is triggered on successful finishes.
- Every drill is now a 60-second Finishing Pitch session.
- Dedicated results metrics and Finishing Pitch HUD copy were added for all six drills.
- Automatic scenario resets keep the session flowing after goals, saves, misses, blocks and failed timing.
