CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V75.11
PLAYER STATUS MOVED INTO A COLLAPSIBLE LEFT RAIL

Starting point
The Match Day Condition and Career Support Staff readouts added in v75.7 and
v75.9 were full-width strips stacked above the training ground. Measured in the
running game at 1600x900, they took 321px of vertical space, which pushed the
training complex below the fold and forced the training centre to scroll before
you could see the pitch at all.

The change
Both readouts now live in a collapsible "PLAYER STATUS" rail down the left of
the training centre, immediately right of the Training Ground / Development nav.
The rail is 216px open and collapses to a 30px vertical tab.

The rendered view is *moved* into its own column rather than re-rendered, so the
handlers bindTrainingGround has just attached (facility clicks, keyboard, the
animation loop) survive the restructure untouched.

Measured in the running game at a 1600x900 viewport with a live career:

  state                              ground w   ground h   needs scrolling
  Before (strips stacked above)          1307        474   yes, 321px of strips above the ground
  Rail open                              1092        474   no
  Rail collapsed                         1278        474   no

So the training centre no longer scrolls in any state, and collapsing the rail
puts the ground back within 29px of its original full width.

Two layout bugs found and fixed while verifying this in the browser:

  1. transition:width on the rail pinned it open. A width transition on a grid
     item sitting in an auto-sized track leaves the computed width stuck at the
     start value, so the collapsed rule never took effect and the rail stayed at
     216px however many times you clicked. Removing the transition fixed it.
  2. The rail could not shrink below its content width because a grid item's
     automatic minimum is its min-content size. min-width:0 was needed.

Neither was visible from reading the CSS - both were caught by measuring
getBoundingClientRect in the running game.

The two readouts reflow for the narrow column: condition rows drop to one
column, staff effects to two, staff chips stack, and both headers go vertical.
Below 1000px the rail returns to a full-width band above the ground.

Verification note
The game cannot be previewed from a file:// or data: origin - the relative
game.js script never loads, so nothing runs. A throwaway static server
(.preview-server.js, git-ignored) was used to serve it over http://localhost so
the layout could be measured for real rather than inferred.
