CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V40 MENTAL ATTRIBUTE USER + AI GAMEPLAY
=================================================================

Mental attributes are now live match systems, not just OVR/profile values.

USER CONTROLLED PLAYER
- Positioning: changes M auto-positioning/support zones, defensive goal-side guidance and onside run discipline.
- Vision: expands passing recognition and improves creative/through-ball target assistance.
- Anticipation: predicts loose balls, moving receivers and second-ball locations earlier.
- Decisions: improves target interpretation, lane selection and onside buffers without overriding manual input.
- Composure: dynamically protects passing/shooting/crossing/first-touch quality when pressed.
- Teamwork: improves supporting angles and spacing around the current ball carrier.
- Work Rate: increases urgency of assisted off-ball movement, with an increased stamina cost.

AI TEAMMATES + OPPONENTS
- Every AI player reads its own seven mental ratings.
- Vision expands scanning/perception and improves receiver prediction.
- Decisions reduce decision delay and improve the quality of AI option selection.
- Anticipation improves reaction time, second-ball assignment and early defensive/attacking reads.
- Positioning and Teamwork strengthen tactical target discipline, support and cover behaviour.
- Teamwork makes one-twos/combination play more likely for suitable players.
- Composure keeps AI technical execution and decision timing more stable under pressure.
- Work Rate increases high-effort pressing/support/recovery activity but drains more stamina.

DESIGN RULE
Manual user input always has final authority. Mental attributes shape interpretation, assistance,
reaction and off-ball intelligence rather than taking control away from the player.
