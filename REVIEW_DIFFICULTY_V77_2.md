# Circle XI v77.2 — Review & Difficulty UX

## Implemented
- Split Step 5 into **Player Review** and **Career Setup** internal tabs.
- Moved Preferred Foot / Weak Foot from Review back into the **Role** step.
- Rebuilt the final dossier around OVR, POT, club, playstyle, football identity, physical profile and international eligibility.
- Added direct **Edit** shortcuts back to Identity, Club, Role and Style.
- Upgraded Player DNA and Academy Scouting Report presentation.
- Career Challenge renamed visually to **Career Journey** with Rising Star, Authentic Journey and Underdog Story.
- Career Type renamed visually to **Career Rules**.
- Match Difficulty now presents Amateur, Professional and World Class while retaining compatible Easy/Balanced/Hard engine values.
- Career Journey and Match Difficulty start unselected; **Begin Career is locked until both are chosen**.
- Added live Starting Path preview for squad role, manager trust, first-team opportunity, reputation and development.
- Added a collapsed Career Journey comparison table.
- Added an Advanced Settings drawer for pass assistance, shot assistance, tutorial prompts and match speed.
- Match Difficulty is identified as changeable later; Career Journey is identified as locked after creation.
- Reduced the right-hand Review player card to six key positional attributes.
- Added sticky final navigation and clearer incomplete/complete messaging.
- Added a final **Your Journey Starts Here** confirmation before the career save begins.
