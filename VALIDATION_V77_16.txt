RUN ANIMATION
  1. Forward lean was dead code. momentumLean was
     computed from acceleration and the gait profile,
     then only ever used as Math.abs(momentumLean)*.18
     on the VERTICAL axis - the lean itself was never
     applied, so a sprinter ran bolt upright. Now
     applied as a real rotation into the direction of
     travel, scaled by how side-on the view is.         PASS

  2. Side-on stride was wrong. The leg cycle's
     'longitudinal' travel was only ever added to the
     figure's local y axis, so running across the screen
     swung the legs UP AND DOWN instead of front to back
     and showed no stride separation. Travel is now
     split between the depth axis and the forward axis
     by |face.x|.                                        PASS
     Verified frame-by-frame over a full 8-phase stride:
     clear front/back scissor side-on where there was
     none before.

     Front and back views are bit-identical by
     construction (sideAmt = 0 -> forward term = 0), and
     confirmed by render.                                PASS

  3. Vertical bob was about 2% of body height, which is
     invisible. Raised toward 5% with a stronger
     airborne lift.                                      PASS
