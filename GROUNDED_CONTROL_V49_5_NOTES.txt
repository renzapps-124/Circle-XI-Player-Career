Circle XI Player Career v49.5 - Grounded Dribbling and Movement

- The visible ball now inherits every player-root movement before smoothing the
  small offset between boot contacts, preventing it from falling behind a runner.
- Loose touches are evaluated at real contact beats and require meaningful stress;
  ordinary unpressured jogging and sprinting are secure.
- Heavy-touch distance and speed have been reduced to recoverable football values.
- Pace and acceleration are applied by one authoritative movement controller.
  The old per-frame physical multiplier that could compound speed has been removed.
- Sharp turns reduce speed, increase directional response and trigger a visible
  turn-plant state. Releasing movement uses stronger braking.
- Running phase is driven by actual root distance so the legs cannot keep cycling
  independently of player travel.
- Skill moves and goalkeeper-rounding now follow short, speed-limited motion paths
  rather than changing the controlled player's coordinates instantly.

All existing match, career, AI, pre-match, first-touch and performance systems are
preserved.
