DEFENSIVE PITCH INTERCEPTION RONDO MOVEMENT V35

Implemented from Defensive Pitch GUI V34.

INTERCEPTION RONDO AI MOVEMENT
- All six possession AI players now move continuously instead of remaining on fixed spots.
- Players reposition around six dynamic perimeter support zones and rotate around the grid.
- Support choices account for the user's position, team-mate spacing and passing-lane quality.
- Ball carriers can move/dribble while scanning, but settle before the kicking contact frame.
- Receivers check towards predicted receiving points and passes lead moving team-mates into space.
- The previous passer immediately performs a short give-and-go / support run after releasing the ball.
- Non-receiving players continue to create new angles while the ball is travelling.
- Occasional half-space movements create third-player combinations without collapsing the rondo shape.
- AI changes support targets when the user closes one side of the grid.
- A stationary watchdog forces a new support run if a non-receiving AI player remains inactive for too long.
- Movement is acceleration-smoothed rather than snapping directly from idle to full speed.
- Existing ground, driven and chipped pass physics remain active.

PASS-AND-MOVE
- Pass targeting now predicts a moving receiver's short-term position in Interception Rondo.
- The ball is led into the receiver's path rather than always targeting their old coordinates.
- Receiver movement is briefly stabilised around the final receiving point so players do not run away from the pass.
- Passing animation still releases the ball at the foot-contact frame.
