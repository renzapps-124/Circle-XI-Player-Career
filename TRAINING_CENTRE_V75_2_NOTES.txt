CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TRAINING CENTRE REWORK (v75.2)
Base: v75.1 Training Session Playthrough Audit

SCOPE
-----
Remove the Schedule and Records tabs from the Training Centre, and rebuild the
Training Ground tab UI/GUI.

1) SCHEDULE AND RECORDS TABS REMOVED
------------------------------------
Both tabs were dead on arrival: their render branches called trainingScheduleMarkup()
and trainingRecordsMarkup(), neither of which exists anywhere in game.js. Clicking
either tab threw a ReferenceError and left the Training Centre blank.

  before: clicking "Schedule" -> Uncaught ReferenceError: trainingScheduleMarkup is not defined
          clicking "Records"  -> Uncaught ReferenceError: trainingRecordsMarkup is not defined

Removed:
  • both nav entries — the Training Centre nav is now Training Ground + Development
  • both render branches
  • the orphaned [data-training-plan-slot] and [data-locate-planned] bindings, which
    only existed to drive the missing schedule markup
  • the dead .schedule-centre / .records-centre header selectors in styles.css

'schedule' and 'records' were added to the view-redirect guard so any persisted or
externally set view falls back to the Training Ground instead of a blank panel.

The one genuinely useful thing the Records tab promised — personal bests — is not
lost: it now appears inline on every drill row in the training-ground zone panel.

2) TRAINING GROUND UI/GUI REBUILD
---------------------------------
The old layout stacked three separate blocks above the pitch (a title header, a
4-cell status strip and a keyboard-legend row), which squeezed the playable explorer
and repeated information the in-pitch prompt already showed.

New layout:

  • COMMAND BAR (.tg-bar) — one compact grid row replacing all three old blocks:
      - identity badge
      - career sessions as three pips (filled = available, dim = used) with a
        "2 of 3 left" / "Practice mode" readout
      - fatigue and sharpness as colour-graded meters (green / amber / red) instead
        of bare percentages
      - Intensity and Difficulty selectors

    Intensity and Difficulty were previously UNREACHABLE. #trainingIntensitySelect and
    #trainingDifficultySelect were only emitted inside the disabled `if(false)` "today"
    branch, so although the change handlers existed and were wired, no player could
    ever reach the controls. They are now in the command bar and persist to the save.
    Intensity feeds development gain and injury risk; Difficulty feeds drill assistance.

  • COACH STRIP (.tg-coach) — surfaces the recommended drill with its facility name,
    plus "Walk there" (moves your player to that facility and opens its panel) and
    "Play now" (launches the drill directly). When training is locked by injury,
    international duty or retirement, this is replaced by a lock strip (.tg-lock)
    explaining the restriction, and the Intensity control is disabled.

  • EXPLORER — now flexes to fill the remaining height instead of a fixed
    `min(530px, 100vh - 296px)`, so removing two rows of chrome gives the pitch the
    space back at every window size. Facility markers gained a drill-count line, and
    the coach's recommended facility gets a pulsing COACH PICK tag.

  • IN-PITCH HUD — the keyboard legend moved off the page chrome and into the pitch
    as a compact chip row (bottom-left), and the context prompt (bottom-right) now
    highlights when you are in range of a zone.

  • ZONE PANEL — each drill row now shows its personal best and grade, or "No personal
    best yet". The coach pick is tagged and highlighted. A session-state banner at the
    top of the panel explains whether you are spending career sessions or in unlimited
    practice mode, and SIM is correctly disabled in practice mode while PLAY stays open.

3) FIXED: TRAINING GROUND ANIMATION LOOP LEAK
---------------------------------------------
bindTrainingGround() held its requestAnimationFrame handle in a local variable, so
every re-render of the Training Ground started an additional loop while the previous
one kept running forever against detached DOM. Each loop redraws the career player
canvas every frame.

  measured before: 1 bind -> 34 frames/s ; 6 binds -> 218 frames/s (~6 loops)
  measured after:  1 bind -> 43 frames/s ; 6 binds ->  43 frames/s (1 loop)

The handle now lives in a module-scoped trainingGroundLoop, stopTrainingGroundLoop()
cancels any previous loop at the start of each bind, and the step function also exits
on its own if its explorer element has been detached.
