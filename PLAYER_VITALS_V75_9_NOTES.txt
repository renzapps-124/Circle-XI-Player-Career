Three of the six never touched a match, which is where a player would most
expect them to matter. Sharpness was the worst: it was written by training, by
playing, and by recovery drills, and then read exactly once, inside
contractEvaluation. Nothing else in the game ever looked at it.

Risk was not a stat at all. It was `fatigue > 65 ? 'High' : 'Low'` — a two-state
readout of one input, sitting next to a real injury model (trainingInjuryChance)
that also weighs fitness, training intensity, training focus, the return-from-
injury doubt window and any medical staff you have hired. You could hire a
Recovery Specialist for GBP 7,200 and cut your injury chance by a third without
the Risk tile moving at all.

1. FITNESS, SHARPNESS AND MORALE NOW REACH THE PITCH
   Added a single match-day condition model where each meter owns one attribute
   group, so the meaning of each one is legible rather than a blended fudge:

     Fitness   -> Physical attributes   (x0.93 - x1.04)
     Sharpness -> Technical attributes  (x0.94 - x1.04)
     Morale    -> Mental attributes     (x0.96 - x1.03)

   Applied to the user's attributes when a match squad is built. AI players and
   quick-sim matches are untouched. Measured on a flat 70/70/70 player:

     vitals                     band            pace  finishing  composure
     Peak (100/100/100)         Sharp             73         73         72
     Healthy (86/77/79)         Sharp             72         71         71
     Flat (60/50/45)            Match ready       70         69         69
     Wrecked (30/25/20)         Below par         67         68         68

   The bands are deliberately narrow. Condition shades a performance; it does
   not replace ability. The full swing top to bottom is 5-6 points, and the
   validation fails if it ever exceeds 12.

   Energy keeps its existing and larger job from v75.3 — the stamina ceiling,
   drain rate, recovery rate and in-match injury multiplier.

2. RISK NOW REPORTS THE REAL MODEL
   Replaced the fatigue coin flip with five bands read straight off
   trainingInjuryChance, with the per-session percentage shown alongside:

     scenario                            band        per session
     Fresh, normal                       Low           0.4%
     Tired, normal                       Moderate      1.6%
     Tired, intense                      High          4.2%
     Spent, intense                      Severe        7.1%
     Spent, intense + medical staff      High          4.0%

   The last row is the point: hiring the Nutritionist and Recovery Specialist
   now visibly moves the readout, which closes the loop with the v75.7 staff
   work.

3. INFORMATION ADDED TO THE RELEVANT TABS
   - Overview: every tile now carries a hover explanation of exactly what it
     drives, plus a live effect tag underneath the value (for example
     "Physical +2%", "Stamina cap 91%", "1st / 2nd choice", "1.6%"). A summary
     line under the tiles states the current match-day condition band.
   - Training Centre: a Match Day Condition strip sits above the staff strip,
     showing all six meters, what each is currently worth, the combined weekly
     training multiplier, and the real per-session injury percentage. This is
     where the meters are actually moved, so it is where the consequences are
     now stated.
   - Contract tab: the agent report gained a Set-piece duty line. Manager trust
     already decided who takes free kicks, corners and penalties through
     setPieceTrustTier, but nothing in the game ever told you that.
