Circle XI Player Career — Defensive Pitch 3D Passing V33
=========================================================

Defensive Pitch passing upgrades

1. Boot-level ball positioning
- Defensive Pitch no longer places a held football at the player body centre/knee line.
- The visible football contact point is projected from the recent Match Day model's actual rig/leg scale.
- Each player gets a boot-line contact anchor that scales with body size, including goalkeepers and differently proportioned squad members.
- Passes render boot-to-boot while their logical pitch coordinates remain centre-to-centre, preserving interception/lane calculations.
- User possession, AI possession and pass reception use the same visual foot-placement system.

2. AI pass variety
- AI can choose ground, driven and chipped passes.
- Pass decisions account for distance, current and projected interception lanes, passer Passing/Vision, defender pressure and risk.
- Blocked ground lanes increase chip probability; narrow open lanes increase driven-pass probability.
- Early-session variety guarantees that both driven and chipped deliveries are introduced instead of relying entirely on random selection.
- Normal AI testing produced ground, driven and chipped passes in the same Interception Rondo session.

3. Driven passes
- Separate driven-pass kick animation.
- Ball release is synchronised to the real kick-contact timing from the shared Match Day animation profile.
- Higher horizontal velocity than a standard pass.
- Very low initial vertical velocity, stronger topspin and flatter trajectory.
- Reduced rolling resistance compared with a normal ground pass.
- Receiving player uses a firmer first-touch/cushion animation.

4. Chipped passes / 3D ball flight
- Chipped passes use x, y and z position plus vx, vy and vz velocity.
- Gravity acts on the vertical axis each update.
- Chipped balls use backspin, air drag and a real rise/apex/descent trajectory.
- Landing applies grass-appropriate restitution and successive bounce damping.
- Backspin slightly holds the ball in flight and reduces forward bounce; topspin makes driven deliveries dip/continue more aggressively.
- The ball shadow stays on the pitch while the ball rises above it, making height readable from the top-down camera.

5. Height-aware defending and control
- Ground passes can be intercepted with the foot.
- Low aerial balls can trigger a raised-foot block.
- Higher reachable balls can trigger a defensive header.
- A chip that is physically above the defender's reachable height is not intercepted simply because its 2D shadow overlaps them.
- Receivers wait for aerial passes to descend to a controllable height and then use an aerial cushion/foot control as appropriate.

6. AI prediction
- Passing-lane risk considers where defenders are moving, not only where they are at the instant the AI decides to pass.
- AI compares receiver distance, lane width and predicted defender movement before selecting the receiver/pass type.

7. Animation/contact synchronisation
- Pass action starts before the ball is released.
- The ball remains at the selected boot during the kicking preparation.
- Release time comes from kickContactTiming() for the actual ground/driven/lofted animation.
- The selected kicking foot is sent to the shared player renderer so foot swing and release side match.

Affected Defensive Pitch modes
- Interception Rondo: full AI ground/driven/chipped passing and height-aware interceptions.
- 3v3 Interception Rondo: the same 3D pass system during defending, securing possession and attacking-role phases.
- 1v1 Defending: held/dribbled ball now uses the same boot-level visual anchor; its empty-goal finishing behaviour remains unchanged.

Existing V32 systems preserved
- Recent Match Day player models.
- Interception scoring and passing-lane scoring.
- 1v1 standing/slide tackles.
- 3v3 press/cover/balance, possession secure phase and role transition.
- A+ remains the maximum training grade.
