CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER – TRAINING GRADE STAT PROGRESSION UPDATE

Implemented requested options 2, 3 and 4.

1. DEVELOPMENT POINTS SYSTEM
- Training grades now award clear Development Points (DP).
- A+ = 100 base DP.
- A = 75 base DP.
- B = 50 base DP.
- C = 25 base DP.
- D = 10 base DP.
- Development Points are banked against individual attributes.
- Each attribute has its own progress threshold before it increases by +1.
- Higher-rated attributes require more DP, so elite players develop more slowly.
- The Development screen now shows current banked DP and the threshold to the next +1.

2. A+ / 3 STAR EXCEPTIONAL BONUS
- A+ or 3-star career training can award an immediate permanent +1 to the drill's primary attribute.
- The bonus is limited to once per drill per training week to prevent repeated farming.
- Free Kick training retains its +1 Free Kicks behaviour through the same unified system.
- Recovery swimming does not award the permanent exceptional attribute bonus.

3. GRADE-BASED SECONDARY ATTRIBUTE UNLOCKS
- D: primary attribute only, small progress.
- C: primary attribute only.
- B: primary attribute + 1 secondary attribute.
- A: primary attribute + 2 secondary attributes.
- A+: primary attribute + up to 3 secondary attributes.
- Secondary attributes are selected from the drill, its training focus and the player's positional development needs.
- Secondary attributes receive smaller DP amounts than the main attribute.

4. RESULTS / GUI
- Training results now explain exactly how the grade affected development.
- Results show the primary attribute, unlocked secondary attributes, DP earned, bank progress and any immediate +1 bonus.
- The Training Centre Development screen includes a permanent grade reward guide.

5. STABILITY
- Added the missing Training Ground decoration renderer discovered during runtime testing so the Training Centre can render safely after a completed drill.
