CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PENALTY TRAINING REVAMP

Implemented directly into Penalty Practice:

GAMEPLAY
- Dedicated penalty aim cursor controlled with WASD / arrow keys.
- Player begins behind the penalty spot with the ball placed separately on the spot.
- Hold/release timing meter with a green PERFECT zone.
- Penalties attribute increases placement reliability and timing forgiveness.
- Composure improves stability under pressure.
- High Penalties + good aim + PERFECT contact becomes extremely reliable without blindly scripting every kick.
- Near timing misses can lead to goalkeeper saves, woodwork or inaccurate attempts.

SHOT TYPES / MATCH-CONSISTENT CONTROLS
- L: Normal penalty.
- J + L: Placed penalty.
- K + L: Low Driven penalty.
- I + L: Panenka.
- Shot types use distinct launch, spin, trajectory and visual kick actions.

GOALKEEPER
- Goalkeeper can commit left/right/centre and at different heights.
- Keeper difficulty rises with pressure.
- Keeper analyses the player's recent penalty placement and can anticipate repeated patterns.
- Panenka logic benefits when the goalkeeper commits early.
- Improved dive/deflection feedback uses the existing match-style goalkeeper renderer.

SESSION MODES
- 10 Penalty Challenge (default).
- Corner Challenge.
- Technique Mastery.
- Shootout Pressure.
- Sudden Death.
- Session selector is available before starting and disappears during live play to keep the pitch uncluttered.

GUI / FEEDBACK
- Compact Penalty Training HUD with Penalties rating, tier and Composure.
- Live goals, attempts, stars and session mode.
- Aim readout and target cursor.
- Technique selector and match control keycaps.
- Live pressure meter.
- GOOD / PERFECT / save / miss feedback.
- Goal corner zones are used as subtle training references.

RESULTS / DEVELOPMENT
- Penalty-specific report showing goals, placement, timing, Composure and technique variety.
- Placement heat map and technique scoring summary.
- Penalties is the primary development attribute.
- High grades unlock Composure, Finishing and Shot Power as secondary development attributes.
- Uses the existing grade Development Points system.
- A+ / 3-star eligible sessions can award Penalties +1 subject to the weekly safeguard.
- Practice sessions do not consume a career training session or farm permanent progression.
