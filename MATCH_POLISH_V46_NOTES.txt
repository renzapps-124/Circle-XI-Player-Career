CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V46 MATCH POLISH / SIMULATION / INJURY PASS

1. Compact player-fitted stretcher
- Deployed stretcher reduced from 68 x 24 to 55 x 19.5 game units.
- Created-player render on the stretcher increased to 1.08 scale and centred on the pad.
- Pillow/strap geometry and medic carry spacing retuned for the smaller frame.
- The stretcher still reuses the actual created player's appearance rather than a placeholder body.

2. Injury presentation
- Injury substitution now has a dedicated medical visual treatment rather than the generic substitution panel.
- Displays match minute, affected area, medical assessment pending status, and the replacement row.
- Continue button now simulates the remaining match instead of instantly ending at the injury score.

3. Rating-driven match simulation
- New shared simulation engine uses attack, midfield, defence, goalkeeper and overall team ratings.
- Used for missed fixtures caused by injury, in-match 'Continue to Full Time', and the pause-menu 'Simulate to Full Time' action.
- Preserves current score/minute for in-progress matches and only simulates remaining time.
- Handles red-card team-count effects, extra time and shootouts.

4. Discipline / yellow cards
- Foul severity is no longer modified by referee card bias.
- A separate disciplinary decision evaluates severity, ball/player contact context, tackle type, angle, speed and persistent fouling.
- Minor fouls are normally no-card events; warnings can occur without a booking.
- Yellow/red cards display a reason (e.g. Late Challenge, Reckless Slide From Behind, Persistent Fouling).

5. Passing teleport protection
- Removed distant loose-ball reclaim and the old pass safety fallback.
- A queued kick only connects while the player still owns the ball or the loose ball is within a 30-unit foot-contact radius.
- Loose-ball kicks use the real ball contact point instead of snapping possession.
- Added a per-frame continuity guard for impossible user-position jumps.

6. Slower AI
- Both team-mate and opponent AI use an additional 0.88 movement scale and 0.84 acceleration scale while retaining individual Pace/Acceleration differences.
- AI movement has a hard per-update continuity cap to prevent large root-motion jumps.

7. Ball weight / gravity
- Airborne gravity increased from 418 to 486.
- Ground rolling resistance increased (Wet 21, Normal 26, Dry 31).
- Low-speed settling threshold increased from 4 to 5.5 so loose balls stop naturally rather than sliding around indefinitely.
