CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — FREE KICK TRAINING OVERHAUL

Implemented in the real Training Centre / Set Piece Area.

FREE KICK MODES
• Free Kick Technique — learn Curl/Bend, Top-Spin Dip and Knuckleball.
• Fixed Target Free Kicks — precision hoops and hanging vest-style goal targets.
• Wall Simulation — match-player wall with live collision and goalkeeper.
• Pressure 10 — 10 consecutive kicks from the same match-realistic position inside a 50-second set.
• Random Match Situations — rotating realistic free-kick angles and distances.
• Free Practice — practise any technique from rotating match-style locations.

TECHNIQUE CONTROLS
• Curl/Bend: select CURL and hold/release L, or use J + L.
• Top-Spin Dip: select DIP and hold/release L, or use K + L.
• Knuckleball: select KNUCKLE and hold/release L, or use I + L.
• Normal: select NORMAL and hold/release L.

PHYSICS
• Curl uses strong side-spin / Magnus-style bend with a wider starting line.
• Top-Spin Dip uses a steeper lift, stronger topspin and accelerated late drop.
• Knuckleball uses minimal spin, higher velocity and controlled lateral / vertical wobble during flight.
• All techniques use live gravity, drag, bounce, post, wall and net collision.
• Match-style ball trails make the different trajectories easier to read without drawing a predicted path before the kick.

MATCH CONSISTENCY
• Training uses the same career player renderer used in matches.
• Wall players use the match footballer renderer instead of mannequin-only placeholders in Wall / Pressure sessions.
• Full-size training goal uses the match-style goal/net presentation.
• Goalkeeper uses the existing training match renderer and reacts to target side and shot height.

TRAINING FEEDBACK
• Tracks goals, wall hits and precision target hits.
• Coach feedback changes based on wall-hit rate and accuracy.
• Pressure 10 ends after 10 attempts and tracks the set live.
• Random / Practice sessions rotate through central, wide-left and wide-right realistic starting spots.
