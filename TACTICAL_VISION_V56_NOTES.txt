CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TACTICAL VISION V56
SCAN → RECEIVE → PLAY UNDER PRESSURE

Implemented on top of v55.

CORE LOOP
- Every repetition now starts with the user OFF the ball.
- An AI feeder owns the ball and automatically plays an incoming pass.
- The user must press M to scan before receiving.
- The ball remains live while scanning; scanning never pauses the simulation.
- The user receives, controls the ball, chooses a teammate and passes.
- The outcome resolves into short feedback and automatically starts a new tactical picture.

SCAN SYSTEM
- New scan timing grades: EARLY, GOOD, PERFECT and LATE.
- Scan information is a temporary snapshot rather than permanent pass assistance.
- Vision affects the amount of information revealed: low Vision identifies fewer options; high Vision exposes more relevant options and danger.
- Missing the scan is scored as NO PRE-RECEIVE SCAN, but the repetition continues rather than freezing.
- Tactical Vision no longer allows queued passes before possession: the user has to scan/receive first, then pass.

DEFENSIVE AI
- AI defenders are split into Presser, Interceptor and Cover roles.
- The presser closes the user while the incoming pass is travelling and accelerates pressure after the first touch.
- The interceptor can attack the incoming passing lane when the geometry is realistic.
- After reception, the interceptor repositions to close the strongest outlet while cover defenders track alternative teammates.
- AI does not read the user's future input; it reacts to lanes, positions, the feeder, the user and available teammates.
- If the presser reaches the user and contact is sustained, possession can be lost with DISPOSSESSED feedback.

PASS OUTCOMES
- Successful passes score decision quality, quick release, progressive intent and whether the pass beat the press.
- Outgoing interceptions show INTERCEPTED · LANE CLOSED.
- Incoming interceptions show PASS CUT OUT / INCOMING PASS INTERCEPTED.
- Decision timeout shows PRESSURE WON and restarts automatically.
- Lost/stalled passes use watchdog recovery and cannot leave the drill permanently stuck.

SCORING / RESULTS
- Tactical Vision result categories now emphasise:
  1. Scan timing
  2. Decision quality
  3. First touch
  4. Speed of play
  5. Pressure resistance
- New tracked metrics include perfect scans, missed scans, quick releases, passes that beat the press, progressive passes, dispossessions and incoming interceptions.

UI / ASSISTANCE
- Permanent J target guidance is hidden in Tactical Vision.
- Passing-lane guidance only appears during the short scan snapshot.
- Defenders identified as immediate dangers receive temporary danger indicators during a scan.
- Incoming pass buttons no longer advertise pass queuing during Tactical Vision.

RELIABILITY
- Dedicated Tactical Vision v56 state chain:
  SCENARIO_SETUP → WAITING_FOR_PASS → PRE_RECEIVE_SCAN → FIRST_TOUCH → DECISION_WINDOW → USER_PASS → PASS_RESOLUTION → FEEDBACK → RESET → NEXT_SCENARIO
- Incoming phase, decision phase and pass-resolution phase all have recovery paths.
- Existing runtime recovery remains in place.
