CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — RECOVERY SWIMMING UPDATE

Implemented in the actual game build:

- Replaced the previous Recovery Centre drill with a dedicated Recovery Pool swimming activity.
- Recovery Pool is accessed from the walkable Training Ground.
- Added three pool sessions:
  • Recovery Swim — 50m active recovery
  • Endurance Swim — 100m controlled recovery
  • Sprint Swim — 50m speed challenge
- Added Play and Simulate options directly at the Recovery Pool.
- Swimming uses J and L as left/right strokes. Alternating the inputs creates the strongest, cleanest rhythm.
- The player is rendered topless in swim shorts while preserving the career player's saved skin tone.
- Pool view uses an overhead lane-based layout with six lanes, lane ropes, pool markings, water effects and other swimmers.
- Added exact swim timer, distance, strokes, rhythm and energy HUD.
- Pace/Sprint Speed determines maximum swimming speed.
- Stamina determines energy drain and ability to sustain speed.
- Recovery Swim reduces career fatigue and improves match readiness rather than applying a normal training fatigue load.
- Recovery sessions have no normal training injury load.
- Simulated swimming results are generated from the player's Pace and Stamina and still apply appropriate recovery benefits.
- Swimming results show time, average speed, strokes, rhythm, energy remaining, fatigue recovered and relevant player attributes.
