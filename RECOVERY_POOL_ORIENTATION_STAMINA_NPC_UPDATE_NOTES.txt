CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — RECOVERY POOL ORIENTATION / STAMINA / NPC UPDATE

Implemented:
- Career swimmer now remains the same drawVisibleFootballer renderer used in matches.
- Fixed pool orientation so the user lies horizontally along the lane rather than upright/vertical.
- The swimmer flips horizontally at each wall so the head always points in the travel direction.
- Recovery Pool stamina has been made less punishing overall.
- Strong stamina scaling added:
  * 90+ Stamina: low stroke cost, fast recovery, minimal need to rest.
  * 75–89 Stamina: manageable drain and short occasional recovery periods.
  * 60–74 Stamina: noticeably higher drain and multiple pacing/rest windows.
  * Below 60: requires deliberate energy management.
- Energy recovery begins sooner for high-Stamina players when stroke frequency drops.
- Full rest regenerates energy substantially faster, again scaled by Stamina.
- Turn recovery is now also strongly Stamina-dependent.
- NPC swimmers no longer use the simplified CSS body model.
- NPC swimmers are rendered using the same match footballer renderer as the user.
- NPCs now inherit squad-style proportions and have varied match-quality skin tones, hairstyles, builds and heights.
- Existing 60-second arcade Recovery Pool, J/L stroke controls, water effects and recovery rewards are preserved.
