CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TRAINING SESSION PLAYTHROUGH AUDIT (v75.1)
Base: v75 Technical Zone Overhaul

METHOD
------
All 40 registered non-hidden training sessions were opened, started, played with live
input and closed inside the real game running in Chromium, driven through the existing
window.__circleTrainingRuntime diagnostic API. Three passes were run:

  1. Boot/teardown smoke pass  — open → start → play → close, per drill.
  2. Randomised-input pass     — 5s of live WASD / J K L I / M / Shift / Space per drill.
  3. No-input soak pass        — 10s per drill with no input, to expose stalls and
                                 runaway scoring loops that only appear when the user
                                 does nothing.

Note: the automated harness replaces requestAnimationFrame with a timer-based shim so
the loops keep running while the browser pane is not compositing. Session countdown
rates measured under the shim are not representative; state-machine transitions,
attempt counts and error counts are.

ISSUES FOUND AND FIXED
----------------------

1) PROTECT THE BALL — runaway failure loop and permanent scenario stall  [CRITICAL]
   technicalProtectUpdate() resolved the physical contest on every frame, without
   checking that the duel was still live. Once t.pressure reached 1 the drill:
     • called technicalScore(..., success=false) every frame, and
     • re-armed t.resetAt to performance.now()+700 every frame,
   so the reset gate `t.phase==='reset' && performance.now()>=t.resetAt` could never
   fire while the defender stayed in contact.
   Measured before fix: 96 attempts / 96 mistakes in 4 seconds, phase locked in 'reset'
   for the rest of the 60 second session.
   Fix: contact resolution is now gated on a `duelLive` flag (phase 'shield' or 'turn'),
   and pressure/hold/stationary are cleared when the duel is lost.
   Measured after fix: 4 attempts in 10 seconds, phases cycling shield → reset → shield.

2) FIRST TOUCH ESCAPE — permanent stall in the 'service' phase        [CRITICAL]
   technicalFirstTouchUpdate() had no watchdog on the 'service' phase. Reception only
   happened when the ball came within 4.7 units of the player, so if the user moved away
   from the pass the ball simply rolled on, decelerated and stopped. The phase never
   advanced and the drill was dead for the remainder of the session.
   Measured before fix: 15 seconds, 0 attempts, ball 53 units away and still in 'service'.
   Fix: technicalSetupFirstTouch() records t.serviceAt, and the service phase now resets
   the scenario with a scored miss when the ball leaves the pitch, when the service has
   been live for more than 5 seconds, or when the ball has come to rest out of reach.
   The reception test is additionally gated on the phase still being 'service' so a
   watchdog reset and a reception cannot both fire on the same frame.
   Measured after fix: service times out at ~5s, scores the miss and cycles normally.

3) LEAKED 'passing-academy-live' CLASS DISABLES THE START BUTTON       [CRITICAL]
   startPassingAcademyTraining() adds `passing-academy-live` to #trainingGameModal, but
   it was only removed in finishControlledTraining() — never in closeTrainingDrill() and
   never on open. Closing a Passing Academy drill early left the class on the modal, and
   the unscoped stylesheet rule

       #trainingGameModal.passing-academy-live #startTrainingGame,
       #trainingGameModal.passing-academy-live #simulateTrainingGame
           { opacity:.42; pointer-events:none }

   then applied to every drill opened afterwards. Reproduced live: with Protect the Ball
   open, the Start button computed to pointer-events:none / opacity:0.42, so no further
   training session could be started until the page was reloaded.
   Fix: closeTrainingDrill() now also clears passing-academy-live, passing-academy-ui,
   gym-bench-ui, race-ui, tactical-training-ui, tactical-setup and tactical-live-session,
   and openTrainingDrillCore() clears every live-session class before setting up a drill.

VERIFIED HEALTHY
----------------
• All 40 sessions open, start, accept input and close with no thrown errors, no console
  errors and no unhandled rejections, in all three passes.
• The v60 training runtime guards (watchdog, recoverable error state, stale-session
  cleanup) never had to engage — no session entered the 'error' status.
• Tactical drills (All Round Circuit, Tactical Vision, Pressing Triggers, Transition
  Game) already carry per-state watchdogs, e.g. PASS and FINISH time out after 6s.
• 1v1 Defending guards its concede path with `att.hasBall`, so it cannot double-fire.
• Off-pitch coordinates seen in the All Round Circuit are correct: that drill runs on
  TACTICAL_ALL_ROUND_PITCH, which is 128 units tall rather than 100.
• Balls leaving the pitch in the generic drills are handled by the existing `settled`
  rule in updateTrainingBall(), which retires the ball after its max life and records
  the attempt.
