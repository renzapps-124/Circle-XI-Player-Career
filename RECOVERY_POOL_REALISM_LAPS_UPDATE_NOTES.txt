CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — RECOVERY POOL REALISM & LAPS UPDATE
13 August 2026

RECOVERY POOL GAMEPLAY
- Recovery Swim is now a 4-lap / 100m session in a 25m pool.
- Sprint Swim is now a 4-lap / 100m session.
- Endurance Swim is now a 6-lap / 150m session.
- Each wall touch records a split time and advances the lap counter.
- Wall turns include a short turn phase, direction reversal, push-off momentum and a small stamina-based recovery window.
- Session HUD now shows current lap, lap progress, total distance, last split, stroke rhythm and energy.
- Results show laps completed, best split, average split and individual lap split times.

SWIMMER MODELS & ANIMATION
- Rebuilt the pool swimmer silhouette with separate head, cap/goggles, torso, swim shorts, articulated arms and articulated legs.
- Career player still preserves the saved skin tone.
- Front-crawl strokes now include body roll and periodic breathing movement.
- Flutter kicks animate continuously and their cycle visually responds to swimming speed.
- User stroke animation remains controlled by alternating J and L.
- NPC swimmers now use the same detailed swimmer component rather than simple bars.
- NPCs receive varied skin tones, shorts and swimming caps.
- NPC swimmers use animated freestyle arms, flutter kicks and realistic end-of-lane turns.

WATER EFFECTS
- Added a more animated pool surface.
- Added wake trails behind user and NPC swimmers.
- Added expanding ripples around moving swimmers.
- J/L strokes create individual hand-entry splash effects.
- NPC swimmers create continuous light splash and wake effects.
- Wall turns trigger a more forceful change in water movement.

PLAYER ATTRIBUTES
- Sprint Speed/Pace continues to determine maximum swimming speed.
- Acceleration continues to influence how quickly swim speed builds.
- Stamina determines stroke cost, passive energy drain and ability to sustain pace over multiple laps.
- Higher Stamina also improves energy recovery during wall turns.

RECOVERY
- Swimming remains active recovery training rather than a normal high-load football drill.
- Completing the session reduces fatigue and improves match readiness.
- Simulate remains available and now produces a full multi-lap result with generated lap split times.
