CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PERFORMANCE GYM PLAYER RIG & ANIMATION V3

Implemented in the Performance Gym:

PLAYER MODEL
- Shared career-player identity is retained across match and training modes.
- Fixed-length articulated player rig used for exercise poses.
- Mechanical elbow/knee circles are no longer visibly drawn; overlapping rounded limbs create cleaner joints.
- Slightly larger, cleaner head with short neck and subtle facial detail.
- More athletic arm proportions, larger hands and cleaner grips.
- Split athletic shorts silhouette that follows the pelvis more naturally.
- More recognisable training shoe shape with heel, toe, sole, laces and player boot accent.
- Pelvis and chest rotation are handled separately for less rigid movement.

EXERCISE BIKE
- Hips remain anchored to the saddle with subtle weight movement.
- Hands remain locked to the handlebars with natural elbow compression.
- Feet remain attached to the pedals through the entire crank rotation.
- Ankle angle changes through the pedal cycle.
- Recovery, tempo/climb, sprint and exhausted postures are blended into the rider animation.
- Subtle hip bob, shoulder movement, head motion and fatigue cues added.

TREADMILL
- Continuous gait blending replaces hard walk/jog/run/sprint animation changes.
- Running cycle now includes contact, compression, push-off and flight behaviour.
- Foot strike is tied to treadmill belt height.
- Forward lean, stride length and knee lift scale with running speed.
- Counter-rotating shoulders and pelvis added.
- Arms swing opposite the legs with a more visible bend.
- Vertical body motion and landing compression give the player more weight.
- Dynamic shadow responds to the running cycle.

BENCH PRESS
- Head, shoulders, torso and hips are positioned on the bench.
- Both feet stay planted.
- Hands stay anchored to the bar while arm IK solves the elbows.
- Bar uses a slight curved path rather than a perfectly vertical path.
- Press animation uses nonlinear easing with a visible sticking-point section.
- Heavy loads and low energy add subtle bar/effort shake.
- Breathing/chest movement added.
- Bar display motion is smoothed between gameplay input increments.

ANIMATION SYSTEM
- Animation phase is advanced using frame delta time, not directly from individual key presses.
- Cadence/speed changes are smoothly interpolated.
- Exercise intensity affects animation rather than only changing HUD values.
- Subtle secondary motion includes breathing, head bob, shoulder movement, hair motion and reactive shadows.
