PERFORMANCE GYM — AGILITY POLE PENALTY V18

- Every unique agility pole hit now deducts 250 points immediately.
- A pole only charges the penalty once per run, preventing repeated overlap from draining points unfairly.
- Live feedback explicitly displays POLE HIT · -250 PTS.
- The live score flashes red when a deduction occurs.
- Pole-contact penalties are accumulated in agility.penaltyPoints.
- The results screen now shows the total pole-contact point deduction and the number of penalised contacts.
- Existing wobble, collision speed loss, moving poles, clean-streak reset and A+ grade cap remain intact.
