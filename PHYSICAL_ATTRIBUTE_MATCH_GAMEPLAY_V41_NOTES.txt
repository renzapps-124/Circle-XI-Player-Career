CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PHYSICAL ATTRIBUTE MATCH GAMEPLAY V41

Implemented from the Profile / Player Creation Physical attributes.

PACE
- Raises the genuine top-speed ceiling in open play.
- Applies both on and off the ball, while Dribbling still controls on-ball retention.

ACCELERATION
- Changes how quickly velocity builds from standing and after direction changes.
- Separates explosive players from players who are only fast once at full stride.

STAMINA
- Changes match-energy drain and recovery.
- Adds a hidden repeated-sprint reserve that drains during sustained sprinting and refills while jogging/walking.
- High Stamina reduces late-match technical/balance penalties and fatigue-related injury risk.

STRENGTH
- Dominates shoulder challenges, shielding and collision force.
- Adds only a small contribution to strike velocity so it does not replace Finishing/Technique.

AGILITY
- Controls direction-change response, turning load and how sharply momentum can be redirected.

BALANCE
- Controls stability in collisions, stumble duration, velocity retention and shielding stability.

MOMENTUM / CONTACT
- Fast contact now matters; Strength determines force while Balance determines stability/recovery.
- High Balance players are less likely to lose the ball from minor collisions.

AI CONSISTENCY
- AI players use the same Pace/Acceleration/Stamina movement ceilings and repeated-sprint reserve.
