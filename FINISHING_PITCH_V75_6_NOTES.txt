CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — FINISHING PITCH V75.6 (DEFECT FIXES)

Scope
Bug-fix pass over the six V74 Finishing Pitch drills. No new drills, no changed
drill identities — the V74 design is kept and the parts of it that did not
actually work are repaired.

1. SHOT RESOLUTION RESOLVED TOO EARLY  (critical)
   updateFinishingPitchBall settled every shot on the first frame the ball
   entered the goal band (b.y <= bottomPct + 1.4) and then latched b.resolved.
   Two consequences:
   - The judgement happened before the ball reached the point it was aimed at,
     so goal-mouth height (encoded in y) was read at the goal-line edge instead.
     Top-corner, top-left/right and high-centre zones were mathematically
     unreachable — targetDist was ~16 units against a 5.4 unit radius.
   - onFrame required b.y <= bottomPct while the trigger allowed bottomPct + 1.4,
     so whether a clean shot counted depended on where the frame step landed.
   Fix: the ball is now resolved on the plane it was actually struck towards.
   The crossing point is interpolated between the previous and current frame
   (b.prevX/prevY/prevZ), so impact x and z are exact and frame-rate independent.
   Measured in FINISHING_PITCH_V75_6_VALIDATION.js with a perfectly aimed shot
   and jittered frame times (240 runs per zone):

     zone            old on-frame  old target  new on-frame  new target
     LOW NEAR               19%        100%          100%       100%
     LOW FAR                19%        100%          100%       100%
     TOP LEFT               24%          0%          100%       100%
     TOP RIGHT              24%          0%          100%       100%
     HIGH CENTRE            21%          0%          100%       100%
     LOW CENTRE             21%        100%          100%       100%

   Woodwork is now resolved from the real goal posts, and over-the-bar,
   wide-of-the-frame and off-target now read back as distinct outcomes.

2. PRESSURE FINISHING WAS UNWINNABLE  (critical)
   During SERVICE the markers moved to 1.5–1.8 units from a stationary receiver,
   and the POSSESSION check ended the attempt at nearest < 2.65 with no grace.
   The attempt therefore died on the first frame after every reception.
   Fix: markers hold a real stand-off while the ball travels (~3.9 units),
   the tackle radius is 1.85, and a defender cannot win the ball until 500ms
   after reception. Close-down speeds are now 7.2–7.8 against a 15–19 user jog,
   so the pressure comes from the short window rather than an instant loss.
   Long-range close-down was rebalanced the same way (11.5 -> 7.8), which gives
   roughly 1.0s to release before the lane closes against a 2600ms window.

3. THE FOOTBALL WAS INVISIBLE FOR MOST OF EVERY ATTEMPT  (critical)
   drawTrainingSharedEngine only rendered the free ball when the drill had no
   team-mates (plus explicit exceptions for corners, defensive and passing).
   Every Finishing Pitch drill spawns a feeder and defenders, so the free-ball
   branch never ran, and the carried-ball branch was gated the same way. The DOM
   fallback ball is class legacy-training-ball, which is display:none!important,
   so nothing drew it.
   The only football that ever appeared was the one drawn at the feeder's boot
   during RESET — which is exactly the reported symptom: the ball vanishes the
   moment it is passed, in all six sessions. Measured by evaluating the real
   render gates out of game.js against each drill phase:

     phase                          before   after
     RESET (feeder holds it)           6/6     6/6
     SERVICE (pass in flight)          0/6     6/6
     POSSESSION (user carrying)        0/6     6/6
     SHOT (strike in flight)           0/6     6/6
     RESOLVED (after the outcome)      0/6     6/6

   Fix: the free-ball gate now covers the finishing pitch, and a carried-ball
   branch draws the football on the user's boot line (same anchor the defensive
   drill uses) while userHasBall is set. Exactly one football is drawn in every
   phase — no double ball during the feeder's hold.

4. THE REQUESTED TECHNIQUE WAS NEVER SHOWN  (major)
   A technique mismatch costs 0.12 quality and makes success impossible, but the
   requested finish was never displayed anywhere. Added finishingTechniqueHint()
   and surfaced it in three places: the illuminated zone marker, the service
   callout and the action guide (e.g. "K+L LOW DRIVEN", "J+K+L KNUCKLE").

5. RESULT METRICS WERE GENERIC  (major)
   trainingResultMetrics had no Finishing Pitch branch, so drill-specific labels
   ("Keeper beaten", "Off-ball movement", "Header timing") were rendered against
   generic accuracy/reaction/energy values, and every counter the drill tracked
   was dead. Added a real branch backed by new tracking: shots, techniqueMatches,
   receptions, lostToDefender, targetHits, timingSamples, powerSamples. blocks
   is now produced too (see 7).

6. ANTICIPATION DID NOT SIZE THE CONTACT WINDOW  (moderate)
   The V74 notes state Anticipation controls the first-time/aerial contact
   window; the code used a fixed 8.5 radius on both the ball and kick sides.
   The window is now lerp(6.4, 10.6) across the attribute range, and first-time
   timing quality is measured against that same window.

7. SMALLER CORRECTNESS FIXES
   - The final timeout branch had no b.resolved guard and could record a second
     attempt for a shot that had already been settled.
   - Lane defenders can now charge a shot down, which is what the previously
     dead "blocks" counter was for.
   - The keeper dived on every strike (gk.action = keeperSave ? 'dive' : 'dive').
     It now stays set for shots that were never going near the frame.
   - Pressure finishing's second defender was gated on s.attempts % 2 === 1,
     which is always false at initialisation. Dead clause removed.
   - 1v1 zone selection indexed the open-zone list with idx % 3, so the fourth
     open zone could never be chosen.
   - The first-time cut-back service was played from y = 18, behind the goal
     line. Moved to y = 27.
   - const window = ... shadowed the global window object inside the ball update.

8. THE DRILL SURFACE HAD NO STYLING AT ALL
   .finishing-goal-target / .controlled-finishing / .finishing-box-line and the
   data-finishing-feedback tone had zero CSS rules, so the "illuminated goal
   zones" rendered as the generic 58px purple movement target with 25px label
   text overflowing it, and the box line was invisible. Added a lit goal-zone
   marker with a readable zone label and technique chip, the penalty/six-yard
   box outline, and success/miss/objective stage tinting. The zone glow animates
   box-shadow only, so it no longer overrides the composure shrink transform
   (CSS animations outrank inline styles, which is why the shrink never showed).

9. TECHNICAL ZONE BALL SAT AT HEAD HEIGHT, NOT AT THE FEET  (major)
   Every technical zone ball placement was written as player + direction * d
   against the player's logical x/y. That anchor is the middle of the body: the
   match renderer draws upright sprites whose boots are footY(11.45) * modelScale
   below it, about 28px on a 760x480 stage. Two placements were worse still and
   set the ball explicitly above the player (player.y - 3.5 for the weak-foot
   stance and the protect spawn, player.y - 2.8 for the weak-foot strike), and
   because every technical setup faces the player up the pitch, the directional
   lead pushed it further up-screen again.
   Result on a body roughly 48px tall:

     case                            before    after
     weak foot stance                  45px      0px
     weak foot strike                  42px      0px
     protect spawn                     45px     11px
     protect shield                    46px     11px
     close control (tight touch)       38px     10px
     first touch (best settle)         43px     15px

   (height of the ball above the boot line). At 42-46px on a 48px body the ball
   was drawn level with the player's head.
   Fix: added technicalBallAnchor(player, lead, side), which starts from the
   same boot-line helper the rest of the training code uses
   (defensiveBallContactAnchor) and then applies the drill's touch distance in
   the direction of travel. Applied to all nine placements: close-control carry
   and checkpoint restart, first-touch settle and escape carry, protect spawn
   and shield frame, weak-foot stance and strike. Standing balls now sit exactly
   on the boot line; carried balls sit a touch distance in front of the boots,
   so a heavy touch still reads as the ball running away from the player. The
   weak-foot ball is also offset towards the weaker foot.
   Touch distance, gate radii and cone collision maths are unchanged — only the
   anchor the offsets are measured from moved.
