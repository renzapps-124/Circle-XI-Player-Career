CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PERFORMANCE GYM RACE BIOMECHANICS V26

Race runner animation/model update based on the V25 model review.

IMPLEMENTED
- Rebuilt Race running around a continuous 0–1 sprint gait rather than a small set of snapping poses.
- Full stride phases: contact/loading, stance, toe-off, recovery, knee drive and contact preparation.
- Added a minimum hip-to-foot reach guard so the knee cannot collapse into highly folded/broken poses.
- Maintained fixed Match Day-derived thigh, shin, upper-arm and forearm lengths.
- Added stance-foot track locking behaviour: the planted foot remains on the track while the pelvis moves over it.
- Added more realistic rear-leg extension and forward knee drive.
- Added centre-of-mass bob/flight movement at high speed.
- Rebuilt Race arms as fixed-length angular chains rather than unconstrained IK.
- Elbows now bend in a controlled sprint direction and stay close to a realistic angle range.
- Upper arms remain visible with clearer shoulder placement and sleeve geometry.
- Opposite arm / opposite leg movement is phase locked.
- Increased Race trainer size by about 20% over V25 and improved heel/sole/toe silhouette.
- Trainers rotate through contact, toe-off, flight and landing preparation.
- Added subtle sole compression on foot contact.
- Added visual pose damping so changes in physical speed do not snap the skeleton between poses.
- Capped animation cadence; higher race speed is expressed with stride length as well as cadence.
- Preserved Match Day appearance/identity for the user and AI teammates.
- Preserved 100m / 200m / 500m Race physics, J/L controls, world-distance movement, camera follow and race GUI.
