CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — ALL ROUND CIRCUIT v51.0
=================================================

LIVE DEFENDERS

- All five defenders now move continuously with pressing, lane-covering and
  penalty-area screening roles.
- Defenders can physically intercept passes and use attribute-driven tackles.
- When a defender wins the ball, the circuit enters a clear TURNOVER state and
  restarts from the first delivery without stopping or resetting the 60-second
  session timer.

INTELLIGENT TEAMMATE RETURNS

- The return passer reads nearby defenders and the active passing lane.
- A clear lane produces a normal grounded return pass.
- One close defender produces a lifted return over the pressure.
- Two close defenders make the teammate carry into a new angle before playing
  a normal pass.
- Return passes lead the user's live movement instead of targeting a stale spot.

PROPER GOAL AND SCORING

- The All Round Circuit now has a full goal line, posts, crossbar and net.
- Post and bar impacts rebound physically and are tracked as woodwork.
- A goal only counts when the ball crosses between the posts and below the bar.
- Successful finishes trigger a visible net ripple and award finishing plus
  circuit-completion points.

LONGER PITCH AND FOLLOW CAMERA

- The circuit uses a 100 × 128 world-space pitch instead of the old fixed square.
- Player and AI movement support the full additional length.
- A damped player-follow camera adds movement look-ahead, keeps play framed and
  subtly tightens for finishing while widening after turnovers.
- Pitch markings, players, ball, goal and tactical overlays all share the same
  camera transform.

PRESERVED SYSTEMS

- Every advanced tactical session remains exactly 60 seconds.
- A+ remains the maximum grade.
- Passing can still be queued before the first touch.
- Existing tactical drills, career rewards, player attributes, shared player
  models, controls and saved progress remain intact.
