CLASSIC MEDIA PLAYER V73 — TORSO / LIMB PROPORTION REBALANCE

Goal
- Correct the remaining disproportion between the torso and limbs across Lean, Athletic and Strong.
- Stop the models reading as a wide torso with overly thin arms and legs.

Changes
- Reworked all three Classic Media body presets around a narrower football-shirt torso.
- Reduced shoulder/chest width compared with V72 for Lean, Athletic and Strong.
- Changed the torso renderer to use one shared straighter silhouette path with a gradual chest-to-waist transition.
- Slightly lengthened the torso by moving the shoulder line up and hip line down, improving head/torso/leg balance.
- Increased upper-arm and forearm thickness on all three builds.
- Increased thigh, knee and calf thickness on all three builds.
- Reduced shorts/pelvis width again so the thicker legs do not recreate oversized hips.
- Preserved short arm lengths from V72.
- Preserved the muscle hierarchy: Lean < Athletic < Strong.
- Strong still gains most of its size through deltoids, arms, thighs and calves rather than an oversized torso.

Build identity
Lean
- Narrowest torso and pelvis.
- Thicker limbs than V72 while remaining streamlined.
- Straight, light footballer silhouette.

Athletic
- Balanced torso width.
- Substantial arms and legs with visible athletic definition.
- Clearly fuller than Lean without becoming bulky.

Strong
- Restrained torso/pelvis width.
- Thickest upper arms, forearms, thighs and calves.
- Muscular silhouette comes from limb/shoulder mass rather than body width.

- Club kit colours/pattern
