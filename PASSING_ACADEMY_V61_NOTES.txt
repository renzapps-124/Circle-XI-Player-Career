CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PASSING ACADEMY V61 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
=================================================================

V61 replaces the simple Passing training route with five live match-style passing sessions while preserving the shared Training Centre runtime introduced in v60.

PASSING GATES
-------------
- AI teammate serves the ball into the user.
- M scan occurs before reception.
- Three live gates are tied to real teammate targets.
- Gate identities use safe/progressive/line-breaking colours, with a blocked red lane.
- Defenders press and close passing lanes.
- The ball must physically travel through the chosen gate and reach the appropriate teammate.
- Gate difficulty, scan, pass quality and quick/one-touch play affect scoring.

ONE TOUCH COMBINATIONS
----------------------
- Compact positional/rondo layout.
- Incoming pass requires pre-receive awareness.
- Quick and first-time passing is rewarded.
- User is asked to move after release before the return phase.
- Teammate combination/return logic continues the sequence without duplicate scoring.
- Defenders press and attempt to cut obvious lanes.

THROUGH BALL TIMING
-------------------
- Attacking-third setup with intelligent runner, defensive line, goalkeeper and visible offside line.
- User reads the run and chooses a ground or lifted through ball.
- Release timing and pass weight determine whether the runner receives in stride, is intercepted or is caught offside.
- Successful creation can continue into a chance/assist reward.

SWITCH OF PLAY
--------------
- Uses almost the full training width.
- Near side is deliberately overloaded while a far winger is free.
- Scan reveals the tactical picture.
- Long ground, driven, lofted and lifted passes can be used.
- A landing zone grades the quality of the switch rather than only whether it reaches a player.
- Receiver momentum and pressure are part of the evaluation.

CROSSING ACADEMY
----------------
- User receives in a wide area.
- Live attackers make box runs while defenders mark and the goalkeeper protects goal.
- Same Passing Academy controls support high/cross, driven and lofted deliveries.
- Landing zone and receiver movement are evaluated.
- Play can continue into a shot/goal/assist reward instead of ending as soon as the cross is kicked.

SHARED SYSTEMS
--------------
- Common Passing Academy state/ownership controller.
- Same user/team-mate kit identity with contrasting defenders.
- Shared ball physics and real physical pass flight rather than target teleporting.
- Match-language pass toolkit: J, K, I, J+K, J+I, K+I.
- Hold/release power.
- Pre-receive scan support.
- Buffered first-time actions where relevant.
- Active defenders and interception logic.
- Dedicated HUD for phase, score, streak, time, scan feedback, pass type and power.
- Outcome locks and reset states prevent repeated scoring or frozen pass states.

FILES CHANGED
-------------
- game.js
- styles.css
- index.html (asset version v61.0.0)

CAREER / TRAINING COMPATIBILITY
-------------------------------
The five sessions continue to run inside the existing Training Centre and do not replace the v60 runtime safety/recovery layer. Existing representative training categories were regression tested after the new passing sessions were added.
