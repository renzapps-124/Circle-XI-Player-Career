CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — 2026/27 WORLD FOOTBALL DATABASE OVERHAUL

DATABASE SEASON
- Database snapshot: 2026/27.
- Verified club database: England, Spain, Germany, Italy and France.
- 360 club records across 18 playable verified divisions/groups.
- Every verified division has a fixed expected club count and validation checks.
- Other countries remain playable through the legacy procedural world generator but are now explicitly labelled GENERATED rather than being presented as verified real-world data.

DOMESTIC LEAGUE DATA
- England: Premier League, Championship, League One.
- Spain: LaLiga EA Sports, LaLiga Hypermotion, Primera Federación Groups 1 and 2.
- Germany: Bundesliga, 2. Bundesliga, 3. Liga.
- Italy: Serie A, Serie B, Serie C Groups A, B and C.
- France: Ligue 1, Ligue 2 and the new professional Ligue 3 Betclic.
- France Ligue 3 Betclic was updated to the FFF's official 18-club 2026/27 field.

LEAGUE CALENDARS
- League seasons now use the actual double round-robin total produced by league size.
- 18 teams = 34 league matches.
- 20 teams = 38 league matches.
- 22 teams = 42 league matches.
- 24 teams = 46 league matches.
- Career calendar, league table, season-end detection and season messages use the correct total rather than a universal 38-match assumption.
- International summer fixtures now begin after the club season instead of forcing week 49 when a longer domestic schedule is still active.

PROMOTION / RELEGATION
- Country and division-aware automatic promotion, play-off and relegation zones.
- England: Championship/League One promotion play-offs and division-specific relegation counts.
- Spain: LaLiga Hypermotion and Primera Federación routes separated.
- Germany: third-place promotion/relegation play-off paths represented.
- Italy: broader Serie B / Serie C play-off routes represented.
- France: Ligue 2 and Ligue 3 Betclic promotion/relegation play-off paths represented.
- User clubs can now be relegated as well as promoted.
- Lowest playable verified divisions do not invent a fake fourth-tier database; the game explains when it has reached the playable verified boundary.

CLUB / KIT DATABASE
- Stable club IDs, country IDs and division IDs.
- Club home identity colours and pattern families stored with each verified club.
- Explicit home, away, third and goalkeeper kit objects generated for every verified club.
- Added support for additional kit patterns including pinstripes, centre stripes, chest bands, contrasting sleeves and crosses.
- Kit clash detection chooses a more suitable away/third kit and separate goalkeeper colours.
- 2026 identity palettes added for major national teams including England, France, Spain, Germany, Italy, Portugal, Ghana, Brazil, Argentina, Netherlands, Belgium, Croatia, Scotland, Wales, Nigeria, Senegal and USA.
- National teams now use their own national kit definitions rather than club colour generation.

IMPORTANT KIT SCOPE
- Verified club home identity colours/patterns are database-backed.
- Where an exact season-specific away/third design is not encoded, Circle XI creates a club-compatible contrasting away/third kit. These are deliberately not labelled as exact licensed replicas.

DOMESTIC CUPS
- Correct domestic cup names attached to the five verified associations.
- England: FA Cup + EFL Cup.
- Spain: Copa del Rey; Supercopa only for qualifying clubs in later seasons.
- Germany: DFB-Pokal; Super Cup only for qualifying clubs in later seasons.
- Italy: Coppa Italia; Supercoppa only for qualifying clubs in later seasons.
- France: Coupe de France; Trophée des Champions only for qualifying clubs in later seasons.
- Super Cups are no longer assigned to every career club.
- Domestic cup qualification routes distinguish Europa League and Conference League places (for example FA Cup vs EFL Cup).

UEFA CLUB COMPETITIONS
- Removed the legacy behaviour that automatically gave every new career a Champions League entry.
- UEFA Champions League, Europa League and Conference League now use separate competition definitions.
- Champions League: 36-team league-phase model, 8 user league-phase matches.
- Europa League: 36-team league-phase model, 8 user league-phase matches.
- Conference League: 36-team league-phase model, 6 user league-phase matches.
- League-phase points, goals, estimated ranking and top-24 progression are tracked.
- Top eight progress directly to the Round of 16; positions 9–24 enter the knockout play-off model.
- UEFA qualifying defeat transfers are modelled from Champions League to Europa League and from Europa League to Conference League where applicable.
- Cross-border UEFA opponent pools use the current provisional 2026/27 UEFA participant lists rather than selecting opponents from the user's domestic league.
- First-season UEFA entry is club-specific.
- Later seasons calculate European qualification from domestic league position, cup success and association performance.
- England/Spain's 2026/27 European Performance Spot concept is represented and future performance spots are simulated from association performance.

LEAGUE TABLE UI
- Champions League, Europa League and Conference League qualification zones are separate.
- Promotion, play-off and relegation zones use the current country's league rules.
- World League Browser now uses those same rules instead of assuming positions 1–4 are continental places everywhere.
- Verified/Generated database status is visible in the World League Browser and career setup.

DATABASE AUDIT
- Added window.CXIFootballDatabaseAudit().
- Added a Database Audit control in the World League Browser.
- Checks expected division sizes, duplicate clubs and missing kit palettes.
- Current static audit: 5 verified countries, 18 verified divisions/groups, 360 clubs, 0 structural errors.

SOURCE BASIS
- Premier League official 2026/27 club/fixture information.
- LALIGA official 2026/27 club lists.
- Bundesliga official 2026/27 promotion/club information.
- Lega Serie A official 2026/27 information.
- Ligue 1/LFP and Fédération Française de Football 2026/27 information, including the official Ligue 3 Betclic field and format.
- UEFA official 2026/27 Champions League, Europa League, Conference League and access-list information.
- UEFA participant lists are still provisional while August 2026 qualifying is ongoing, so the database identifies the 2026/27 snapshot and does not pretend the final 36-team fields have already been determined.
