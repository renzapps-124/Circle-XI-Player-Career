CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER - TREADMILL BIOMECHANICS V12

Implemented treadmill-specific animation and model refinement:

- Rebuilt treadmill gait around stance, loading, push-off, recovery, knee drive and preparation phases.
- Added speed-dependent Walk, Jog, Run, Fast Run, Sprint and Max Sprint gait profiles.
- Physical treadmill speed and visual gait speed are now separated so acceleration blends smoothly instead of snapping poses.
- Added acceleration-specific forward lean and smoother deceleration posture recovery.
- Forward-only knee pole guidance remains active with fixed thigh and shin lengths.
- Improved support-load, landing compression, flight and centre-of-mass movement.
- Feet are anchored above the treadmill deck by the trainer sole height so shoes contact the belt instead of clipping through it.
- Trainers are 36% larger than the canonical side-rig shoe scale for clearer proportions on the treadmill.
- Added trainer sole detail and subtle sole compression on foot strike.
- Added treadmill-specific hand paths and elbow pole guides for more natural arm swing.
- Shorts now have a stable pelvis/waist section plus independent thigh-following leg openings.
- Each shorts leg overlaps the top of its corresponding thigh so the legs no longer look detached from the clothing.
- Treadmill console now explicitly labels and displays YOUR SPEED using the same live user-speed value used by gameplay.
- Belt marker speed is driven from the same live speed value as the treadmill display.
- Existing J/L rapid alternation gameplay and Stamina/Pace/Acceleration development are preserved.
- A+ remains the maximum training grade.
