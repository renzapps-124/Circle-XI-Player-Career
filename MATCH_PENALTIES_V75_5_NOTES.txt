CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — MATCH PENALTY REBALANCE (v75.5)
Base: v75.4 Squad / Attribute Editor / Match Audio

REQUEST
-------
Make match penalties easier to score when aimed well; make AI opponents and AI
team-mates score more often; make low penalty ratings produce more misses, woodwork
and goalkeeper saves.

THE HEADLINE PROBLEM: A MATCH PENALTY COULD NOT MISS
-----------------------------------------------------
Three separate clamps made an off-target penalty geometrically impossible at any
rating, for the user and for the AI alike:

  • finalAimX was clamped to .025-.975, which maps to a goal-line target of 271-409.
  • gy was then clamped a second time to goalTop+5 .. goalBottom-5 (270-410).
  • target height was lerp(30, 3.5, finalAimY), so the ball topped out at 30 units
    against a 43-unit crossbar.

The goal is scored on y in (270, 410) and z < 40. Every penalty therefore arrived
inside the frame by construction. The only outcomes the engine could produce were
"goal" and "keeper save" — never wide, never over, effectively never the woodwork.

Worse, the old numbers actively punished good placement. Modelled at the shipped
formulas, aiming down the middle scored MORE than aiming at a corner:

  BEFORE, user, perfect timing        GOAL    SAVE   WOOD    OFF
    corner aim, rating 30            51.6%   48.4%     0%     0%
    corner aim, rating 90            63.0%   37.0%     0%     0%
    centre aim, rating 60            72.1%   27.9%     0%     0%   <- best option
  BEFORE, AI taker
    rating 30                        50.4%   49.6%     0%     0%
    rating 60                        57.2%   42.8%     0%     0%
    rating 90                        96.0%    4.0%     0%     0%

WHAT CHANGED
------------
1) The frame is now reachable from outside. The second gy clamp opens to
   goalTop-46 .. goalBottom+46, the aim clamps allow overshoot (user -.17..1.17,
   AI -.16..1.16), and the height map became piecewise: inside the aim range it
   spans 34 down to 0, and a negative finalAimY (an overhit) climbs at 74 per unit,
   which clears the 43 crossbar. A deliberate top-corner aim still lands at ~32 and
   stays under the bar; only genuine scatter puts it over.

2) Placement is rewarded. aimQ (how far from the middle you pointed) went from 15%
   of the quality mix to 24%, and the error band widened at the bottom and tightened
   at the top: lerp(.078, .0045) became lerp(.205, .0075).

3) Low ratings spray it. That same wider error band means a rating-30 taker sits
   ~2.3 standard deviations from the near post on a corner aim, where a rating-99
   taker sits ~9. Misses, posts and saves all fall out of that naturally rather than
   being special-cased.

4) AI takers place it better. This single branch serves opposition penalties and the
   user's own team-mates, so both improved together: corner offset lerp(.20,.42) ->
   lerp(.24,.415), scatter lerp(.075,.018) -> lerp(.155,.040). The elite end was
   pulled marginally in from .42 to .415 so top takers stop aiming at the post itself.

5) The keeper is beaten by a good penalty. Side-read chance dropped from
   clamp(.23 + …, .09, .50) to clamp(.185 + …, .06, .42), a strong taker suppresses
   the read harder (.003 -> .0042 per point), and the taker-threat reach penalty rose
   from 4.4 to 7.4.

Aiming at a corner is now clearly better than the middle at every rating, which is
the opposite of the old behaviour. Rating drives conversion across a ~30 point range
for AI takers and ~19 points for the user. Woodwork and off-target both appear and
both fall away as the rating climbs.

SCOPE NOTE
----------
The Penalty Practice TRAINING DRILL is deliberately untouched. performPenaltyTrainingKick
uses the same expression shapes as the match code and sits earlier in game.js, which is
an easy thing to edit by accident — the validation script scopes its scrape to
takePenaltyTrainingSetPiece for exactly that reason.

HOW THE NUMBERS WERE PRODUCED
-----------------------------
MATCH_PENALTIES_V75_5_MODEL.js reproduces the shipped aim formulas and goal geometry
by scraping the live constants out of game.js, then runs 40,000 penalties per cell.
Run it against any copy of game.js:

    node MATCH_PENALTIES_V75_5_MODEL.js game.js

It is a model of the aim and geometry, not a full engine simulation. The parts it
models exactly are the ones the constants control: aim distribution, where the ball
crosses the line, its height there, and whether that is inside the frame. The keeper
is approximated — a committed dive covers a band on the guessed side and leaves the
centre open — using the engine's own read probabilities and reach formula. So treat
the absolute save percentages as indicative and the goal/woodwork/off-target
structure and the before/after direction as solid.

The one hard, non-modelled fact: before this change the clamps made off-target and
woodwork outcomes impossible, and after it they are not.

NOT DONE
--------
No live in-match measurement. The match screen saturates this environment's renderer
(requestAnimationFrame runs at roughly 1 fps with the pane composited), so the match
clock barely advances and a penalty restart never reaches its 'ready' phase. The
changes are arithmetic inside existing code paths and add no new API surface, but the
conversion rates above have not been confirmed against the running engine — worth a
few penalties in a real match to sanity-check the feel.
