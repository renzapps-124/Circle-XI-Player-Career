CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PERFORMANCE GYM COMPLETE REVAMP

Implemented from the latest Corner Real Contact & Scoring build.

NEW PERFORMANCE GYM MINI-GAMES

1. EXERCISE BIKE
- 60-second cadence and resistance mini-game.
- Alternate J and L to pedal left/right.
- RPM responds to rhythm, timing, Stamina and Work Rate.
- Interval phases: Warm Up, Tempo, Sprint, Climb, Final Sprint.
- Resistance changes automatically between phases.
- Compact live HUD: target RPM, current RPM, resistance, score, time, energy.
- Same-key presses and poor rhythm lose cadence.
- Stamina reduces energy drain; Work Rate improves sustained intensity.
- Development restricted to Stamina (primary) and Work Rate (secondary).

2. BENCH PRESS
- Controlled 6-rep strength mini-game.
- Hold K to lower the bar; release around the ideal chest-depth timing.
- Press J + L together repeatedly to drive the bar to lockout.
- Real bar position moves through lowering and push phases.
- Load choices: Light, Working Set, Heavy, Personal Best.
- Available weights scale from the player's current Strength.
- Heavy/PB loads produce more difficult upward movement and fatigue.
- Rep feedback: Perfect Lower, Good Control, Rushed Rep, Perfect Rep, Strong Rep.
- Development restricted to Strength only.

3. TREADMILL INTERVALS
- 60-second pace/incline mini-game.
- W increases treadmill speed; S decreases speed.
- Hold Shift for sprint effort.
- Interval phases: Jog, Run, Sprint, Incline, Recovery, Final Sprint.
- Target speed adapts to the player's Stamina level.
- Incline and target pace change automatically.
- Running belt animates during live play.
- Low energy makes excessive pace harder to sustain.
- Development restricted to Stamina (primary) and Work Rate (secondary).
