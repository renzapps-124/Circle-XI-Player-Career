CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — PERFORMANCE GYM AGILITY RUNNING V17

Agility drill redesign
- Removed the football from Agility gameplay and rendering. This is now a pure running/change-of-direction drill.
- The player runs through a tighter 10-pole slalom using WASD.
- Pole spacing has been reduced substantially compared with the previous seven-pole course.
- Flexible poles retain soft collision detection: contact bends/wobbles them instead of acting as a hard wall.
- Every pole now also oscillates vertically with staggered speed, amplitude and phase so the course is dynamic rather than synchronized.
- Pole collision wobble is layered on top of the vertical movement.

Attribute-driven movement
- Pace sets the player's maximum running speed.
- Acceleration controls how quickly the player reaches/regains that speed.
- Agility controls turn response and how much momentum is retained through sharp cuts.
- Balance controls how much speed is retained after pole contact.
- Sharp direction changes deliberately reduce momentum, then Acceleration drives the recovery.
- No Shift sprint modifier is required in this drill; speed comes from the player's attributes and continuous movement input.

Course & scoring
- Ten close poles with multiple course variants.
- Clean cuts, perfect tight cuts, retained momentum and running speed all contribute to score.
- Pole contact breaks the clean streak and applies a small speed/score penalty.
- The final section becomes a straight sprint so Pace has a clear visible role.
- A+ remains the highest grade.

GUI
- Live TIME, SPEED, SCORE, CLEAN and STREAK chips.
- 10-pole course-progress strip plus final-sprint marker.
- Live speed uses the same actual movement speed that drives the player.
- Live run-quality feedback: RESET & DRIVE / BUILDING SPEED / EXPLOSIVE / MAX VELOCITY.
- Ball/dribbling prompts and ball-control metrics removed.
