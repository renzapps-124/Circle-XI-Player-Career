Circle XI Player Career — Performance Gym Treadmill Intervals V8

Implemented:
- Replaced the treadmill-specific player with the shared career-player rendering identity used by Match Day / swimming.
- Fixed backward-bending treadmill knees with forward pole-guided two-bone leg IK and fixed bone lengths.
- Added an 8-phase running cycle: strike, compression, mid-stance, push-off, flight, knee drive, preparation and next strike.
- Added speed-dependent stride length, knee lift, forward lean, counter arm swing, torso/pelvis rotation, head stabilisation, ankle articulation, breathing and sprint effects.
- Treadmill gameplay now requires rapid alternating J/L inputs. Repeated same-side presses do not build proper sprint rhythm.
- Added live stride rate, J/L combo, target-reach and target-hold feedback.
- Added six interval stages: Warm Up, Build, Sprint 1, Recovery, Sprint 2 and Final Sprint.
- Pace influences maximum sprint speed, Acceleration influences how quickly the player builds speed, and Stamina influences sustained high-intensity performance.
- Added gradual acceleration/deceleration, fatigue and end-of-session cooldown animation.
- Added treadmill-specific scoring based on interval clears, time in target zone, stride rhythm, sprint speed and target-reach performance.
- Maximum possible grade is A+; there is no grade above A+.
- Completed treadmill sessions award development toward Stamina, Pace and Acceleration, scaled by grade/performance.
- Added treadmill-specific A+ results report: peak speed, intervals cleared, perfect strides, fastest target reach, time at target, longest streak and peak presses/sec.

Validation:
- game.js passes Node JavaScript syntax validation.
- Runtime pose audit sampled a full high-speed sprint sequence with both knees remaining forward.
- Fixed thigh/shin lengths reported zero leg stretch across sampled frames.
- A+ result path was validated with Stamina, Pace and Acceleration development rewards.
