Circle XI Player Career — Defensive Pitch GUI V34
===================================================

- Rebuilt the live HUD into three compact zones: player attributes, current objective, and live session stats.

- Simplified the right sidebar to grade, objective, readable club leaderboard, concise controls, and Start.

- Normal phase information stays in the top-centre objective chip; event banners only appear for success/miss feedback.
- Cleaner navy/cyan/yellow colour hierarchy with red reserved for mistakes.
- Leaderboard now shows a PB dash before the user has a score instead of 'You 0'.
- Interception terminology is consistent across the Defensive Pitch interface.
- Existing V33 boot-level ball placement and 3D ground/driven/chipped pass physics are preserved.

- Normal training prompt is hidden outside contextual success/miss toasts, keeping the centre of the pitch clear.
