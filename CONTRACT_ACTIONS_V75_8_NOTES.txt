CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V75.8
CONTRACT ACTIONS: THE FOUR BUTTONS NOW DO SOMETHING

Starting point
Of the four buttons on the Contract Actions card, one was correctly gated, one
opened a browser alert(), and two were repeatable flavour buttons that quietly
handed out unlimited stat gains.

1. "TALKS OPEN AT NEXT REVIEW"  (was: correctly disabled, but uninformative)
   The button told you talks were shut without saying when they open, and the
   banner underneath only named a vague period. contractNegotiationWindow() now
   returns nextWeek, and the button reads "Talks open in week 21" (or 39, or 1)
   with a tooltip naming the review it belongs to.

2. "ASK AGENT FOR ADVICE"  (was: a browser alert with three canned strings)
   Replaced with a real in-game Agent Report panel on the contract screen, built
   from live career state rather than a score threshold:
   - manager approval out of 100, colour-coded
   - recent form average
   - current squad role
   - negotiation window status and the week it reopens
   - your wage against the offer the club would realistically make right now
   - the best formal offer on the table (Elite Agent only)
   plus written advice that names your strongest approval component, the one
   holding the deal back, what a renewal is worth per week, and when to ask.
   Hiring the Elite Agent from the Finances tab upgrades the panel and its
   advice, which gives that hire a second visible use beyond contract maths.
   Consulting the agent is written into negotiation history.

3. "EXPLORE LOAN OPTIONS"  (was: a canned inbox message and +2 morale, forever)
   The old handler posted "Your agent will assess clubs that can promise regular
   minutes", gave +2 morale, and nothing ever came of it. It could be clicked
   indefinitely for free morale.
   There is now a working loan market built on the existing transfer
   infrastructure:
   - Eligibility is checked first. A Key Player or Regular Starter is told no
     club will take them on loan; so is a player over 28, a player with under
     half a season left on their deal, and a player already out on loan.
   - Scouting draws from the real club database (allTransferClubs), filtered to
     clubs at or below your own reputation, ranked against your overall, and
     returns up to three clubs with a promised role, a wage and a length that
     runs to the end of the season.
   - Offers render as cards on the contract screen with an Accept Loan button.
   - Accepting moves you to the loan club through the same club-move code path
     that permanent transfers use (extracted into applyCareerClubMove so both
     share it), snapshots your parent club, league, wage and squad role, and
     applies the loan wage and role.
   - The loan clock ticks on the same weekly hook that settles wages. When it
     runs out you are automatically returned to your parent club with your
     wage, squad role and league table restored, and the spell is written into
     negotiation history.
   - The search is limited to once per week.

   Replay from the validation harness (Rotation player, OVR 66, week 8,
   parent club reputation 64):

     Loan offers found              3
     Loan length (weeks)           38
     Wage while on loan          1650
     Wage after return           2000

4. "STAY FOCUSED"  (was: +1 manager trust per click, unlimited)
   Manager trust is 20% of the contract evaluation score, so this button could
   be spam-clicked to 100 trust and used to force contract talks open. It is now
   a once-per-week commitment worth +1.5 manager trust, +1 morale and +1 training
   sharpness, logged to negotiation history. Clicking again in the same week
   tells you the manager has already noted it.
