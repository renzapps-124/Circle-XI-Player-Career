FIRST MATCH

Control only your player

Move and aim with WASD. Hold and release J to pass, K for a through ball, L to shoot, or I to cross—the longer you hold, the more power you use. Passing and shooting are semi-assisted, while crosses target dangerous teammates; player attributes determine the strength and accuracy of the assistance. Hold Space to jockey without the ball or shield while dribbling. When defending, hold I to pressure the ball carrier, use K to block, L for a sliding tackle, and J to send the nearest teammate to press while you cover. Each defensive action uses its own situation-based animation. Goalkeepers use J to dive right, L to dive left, K for low middle and I for high middle. Combine J or L with K or I for low and high corner dives. Shift adds driven passes, finesse shots and lofted deliveries. Fouls, cards, free kicks, penalties, first-time actions and substitutions are active.

MATCH INTELLIGENCE

Play like a footballer, not just a controller

⌖
Keep your position

Follow the coloured positioning guide. Good movement improves rating and manager trust.

!
Call for the right ball

When a teammate has possession, J asks to feet, K requests space, L tells them to shoot and I requests a cross.

◈
Defend with timing

Space jockeys, K tackles, L slides and Q challenges shoulder-to-shoulder. Standing, sliding and physical tackles each use three situation-based animations. Mistimed actions can cause fouls and cards.

▮
Manage energy

Sprinting drains stamina. Empty energy increases injury risk and can make the manager substitute you.

↑
Read lofted balls

Follow the moving shadow and landing marker. Use J, K, L or I to choose your header.

✓
Follow instructions

Your role changes what the manager expects. The post-match review explains strengths and weaknesses.
