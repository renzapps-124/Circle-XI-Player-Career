Circle XI Player Career v49.4 - Match Performance Update

This update reduces match-day lag while preserving gameplay, animation, AI, career,
pre-match presentation, dribbling and first-touch systems.

Main improvements
- Adaptive rendering lowers internal pixel density only when a sustained frame-time
  problem is detected, then restores quality when performance recovers.
- Low and stressed Auto modes render the canvas at 30 FPS while keeping the match
  simulation and input polling on requestAnimationFrame.
- Fixed-timestep catch-up is bounded so a slow frame cannot trigger a spiral of
  excessive simulation work.
- Players and animated crowd members outside the camera view are no longer drawn.
- Crowd and weather particles now respect the selected detail settings.
- Full-screen atmosphere gradients are cached instead of rebuilt every frame.
- Duplicate rain and snow rendering has been removed.
- Match HUD and clock updates are throttled and DOM writes are cached.
- Costly live-canvas backdrop blur is disabled in Auto and Low performance modes.
- Replay sampling is reduced from 30 to 20 FPS with a smaller bounded history.
- Net meshes are captured for replay only while either net is moving, avoiding more
  than 300 unnecessary node snapshots per idle replay frame.
- Replay history is pruned in batches to reduce repeated array shifting.

Controls, match rules, AI decisions and animation timing remain unchanged.
