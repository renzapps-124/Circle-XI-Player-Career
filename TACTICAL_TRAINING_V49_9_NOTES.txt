CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TACTICAL PITCH TRAINING v49.9
=======================================================

FOUR PLAYABLE PITCH DRILLS

1. All Round Circuit
   Receive different deliveries, control the physical ball, play into a moving
   teammate, make a supporting run, receive the return and finish.

2. Tactical Vision
   Scan before receiving, read temporary pressure and lane information, then
   choose an optimal or acceptable real passing option while the ball remains live.

3. Pressing Triggers
   Identify real and fake triggers, hold shape when required, and curve the press
   to screen the central lane. Outcomes include direct regains, forced backwards
   passes, clearances, delays and broken presses.

4. Transition Game
   Continuous small-sided play that switches between attack and defence whenever
   possession changes. Break forward, support, recover centrally, cover lanes and
   track the most dangerous runner.

CONTROLS

- W A S D or Arrow Keys: Move / aim
- Shift: Sprint
- J: Pass
- K: Through pass
- I: Cross / lofted pass
- L: Shoot
- M: Scan
- Space: Press / tackle

SESSION OPTIONS

- Standard: Full normal session with career rewards
- Score Attack: 90-second high-score session
- Endless: Four-minute extended run
- Practice: No career reward or tactical XP, preventing reward farming
- Difficulty: Beginner, Easy, Normal, Hard and Elite
