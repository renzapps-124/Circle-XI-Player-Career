FREE KICK SET PIECE REBALANCE

Implemented changes:
- Removed Pressure 10 from Free Kick Training.
- Free Kick Training now focuses on: Free Kick Technique, Fixed Target Free Kicks, and Wall Simulation.
- Controls/shot types are now consistent across all free-kick training activities.
- Fixed Targets now rotate through real required techniques: Curl, Top-Spin Dip, Knuckleball, and Normal.
- Dummy walls in Technique and Fixed Target modes are smaller (visual scale and collision radius reduced) so shots can travel over the wall more easily.
- Stat-based free-kick assistance has been rebalanced:
  * Technique + Fixed Targets:
    - 90+ Free Kicks: highly reliable for Curl, Top-Spin Dip, Knuckleball, and Normal.
    - Low Driven is excluded from that elite assistance in those two modes.
    - 80+: sometimes score.
    - 70+: less likely.
    - 60+: very rare.
  * Wall Simulation:
    - 88+ Free Kicks: more likely to score with all shot types, including Low Driven.
- Elite assistance improves contact quality, power matching, miss radius, and bend consistency rather than scripting goals.
