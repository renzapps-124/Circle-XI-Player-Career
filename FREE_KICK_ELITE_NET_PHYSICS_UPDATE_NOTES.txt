FREE KICK ELITE + NET + PHYSICS UPDATE

Implemented:
- Stronger free-kick stat influence so elite takers feel elite without teleporting the ball into the goal.
- 90+ Free Kick Accuracy in Technique / Fixed Targets gets strong power, contact and aim correction.
- 95+ becomes near-master execution with very small placement error while preserving live ball physics.
- 88+ Wall Simulation assistance remains strong for every available technique, including Low Driven.
- Goalkeeper visual reads are less perfect against elite Dip and Knuckleball strikes.
- Smaller training dummies now have matching scaled collision height, so shots that visually clear them actually clear them.
- Free-kick training goal rebuilt with a deeper square-mesh back net, roof net and side netting.
- Net impact is local to the ball-hit location and reacts differently by shot type.
- Curl, Top-Spin Dip, Knuckleball, Normal and Low Driven now use dedicated match-renderer kick animation profiles.
- Curl: wider inside-foot sweep, stronger lateral bend and controlled dip.
- Top-Spin Dip: higher launch, delayed late dip and sharper upward strike motion.
- Knuckleball: compact punched follow-through and irregular mid-air direction changes.
- Normal: cleaner direct power strike with moderate lift.
- Low Driven: flatter, faster flight and compact low follow-through (Wall Simulation only).
- Existing consistent J/K/I/L free-kick controls are preserved.
