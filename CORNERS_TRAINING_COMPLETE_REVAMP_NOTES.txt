CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — CORNERS TRAINING COMPLETE REVAMP

Implemented two distinct Corner Training paths:

1. TAKE THE CORNER
- Delivery selection: Inswinger, Outswinger, Driven, Floated and Near-Post Whip.
- Hold/release cross power for delivery control.
- Target zones include Near Post, Centre/Penalty Spot, Far Post and Edge of Six-Yard Box.
- Corners, Crossing, Technique and Vision influence delivery quality.
- Delivery types use different height, pace and curve profiles.
- 10-attempt session flow and corner-specific feedback.

2. ATTACK THE CORNER
- WASD movement and free positioning inside the penalty area.
- AI corner taker serves different deliveries.
- Defenders track and mark the controlled player.
- Contextual finishing options:
  J = Header
  L = Volley
  K = Half Volley
  I = Bicycle Kick
- Ball height, bounce state, distance, pressure and player attributes determine contact quality.
- Bicycle kicks require sufficient Technique and Agility.
- Near-post, far-post, central, late and back-post run targets are supported.
- Match-style goalkeeper dive reactions and goal/net reactions are retained.

TRAINING PROGRESSION
- Corner Delivery uses Corners as the primary Development Point attribute.
- Delivery secondaries include Crossing, Technique, Vision, Curve and Composure.
- Attack mode dynamically uses Finishing or Heading as the primary attribute depending on the techniques used.
- Attack secondaries can include Heading, Finishing, Anticipation, Off the Ball, Jumping, Strength, Technique and Composure.
- Existing grade progression rules remain active: A+, A, B, C and D award Development Points.
- A+ / 3-star exceptional +1 reward uses the existing weekly safeguard.

GUI
- Dedicated Corner Training interface with Take the Corner / Attack the Corner selector.
- Compact live HUD and technique selectors.
- Delivery and finishing controls are shown contextually.
- Live sessions hide unnecessary sidebar UI and expand the pitch.
- Corner-specific results report shows mode, success rate, goals, streak and technique usage.

VALIDATION
- JavaScript syntax checked with node --check.
- Final ZIP integrity checked before delivery.
