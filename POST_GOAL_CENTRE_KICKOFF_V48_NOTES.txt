POST-GOAL CENTRE KICK-OFF - V48 NOTES
=====================================

Requirement
-----------
After a goal, the team that conceded restarts from the centre spot with a kick-off.

What was already correct
------------------------
The main path was in place and verified in a live browser:

  goal() -> goalCelebration{kickTeam: 1 - scoringTeam}
         -> celebration timer expires -> goal replay prompt
         -> watch replay / continue -> setupKickoff(kickTeam, {afterGoal:true})

setupKickoff() resets every active player to their formation slot, parks the ball on the
centre spot and starts a 'kickoff' restart; arrangeRestart() then keeps each side in its own
half and clears the centre circle apart from the taker.

Defects fixed (game.js)
-----------------------
1. A running referee advantage could hijack the kick-off.
   startRestart() rejects or re-routes any restart while an advantage is live. With an
   advantage still on the books for the side that had just scored, the post-goal kick-off was
   converted by callBackAdvantage() into a FREE KICK for the scoring team, roughly 125px off
   the centre spot, with five players in the wrong half. A kick-off is fixed by the laws and
   the ball is dead, so it now clears any advantage instead of deferring to it.

2. setupKickoff() ignored whether the restart was actually created.
   A refused startRestart() left playState on 'RESTART' with this.restart === null, which
   falls through to open play - i.e. the match resumed from a loose ball on the centre circle
   with no kick-off. It now retries with every veto suppressed, and only drops to open play
   if that also fails.

3. The replay prompt handlers depended on the celebration object.
   watchGoalReplay() and skipGoalReplay() both early-returned unless this.goalCelebration was
   still set, so if it were cleared while the prompt was up, the buttons and the Enter/Esc/
   Space keys all went dead and the match froze with no kick-off. The restarting team is now
   stored on the match (pendingKickoffTeam) when the prompt is shown, and goalRestartTeam()
   reads it from either source.

4. The replay prompt had no timeout.
   If the answer never arrived (lost focus, swallowed input) the match sat on the prompt for
   ever. goalReplayPromptTimer auto-continues to the kick-off after 20s. It is long enough
   never to interrupt a deliberate choice.

5. setupKickoff() now clears the celebration, the prompt and any advantage up front, so
   nothing from the previous passage of play can survive into the restart.

Verification
------------
Driven through the real engine in a browser (index.html?demo=tackle-fix-v45), stepping the
fixed-timestep update loop. Every case ends with type 'kickoff', the conceding team as
restart.team, the ball on the centre spot (525,340), 0 players in the opponent's half and
0 players other than the taker inside the centre circle:

  user scores                              -> kick-off to team 1
  AI scores                                -> kick-off to team 0
  prompt left unanswered (20s watchdog)    -> kick-off to team 1
  celebration object lost while prompt up  -> kick-off to team 0
  stale advantage for the scoring team     -> kick-off to team 1  (was: free kick, wrong side)
  stale advantage for the conceding team   -> kick-off to team 0
  watch replay, then replay ends           -> kick-off to team 1
  kick-off taken                           -> OPEN_PLAY, ball leaves the centre spot

Pre-match, second-half and extra-time kick-offs were re-checked and are unchanged.

Not touched
-----------
preview.html, preview2.html, preview_head.html, _runtime_penalty_demo.html and
__inline_test.html carry their own inlined copies of the old engine. They are debug snapshots,
not the shipped app, so they still contain the old behaviour.
