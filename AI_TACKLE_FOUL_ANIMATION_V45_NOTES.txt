CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — V45 AI TACKLE & FOUL ANIMATION FIX

AI SLIDE TACKLES
- Added a dedicated committed slide state: plant -> slide -> recovery.
- AI predicts one contact point when it commits and cannot home toward the ball afterwards.
- Normal pathfinding is locked during the slide and recovery.
- Slide velocity decays through pitch friction and is hard-capped to a short maximum distance.
- Player collision separation no longer drags/teleports a committed slider across the pitch.
- Missed slides finish their existing slide/recovery sequence instead of starting a second slide animation.
- AI slide decisions now use Tackling, Anticipation and Decisions confidence.
- Sliding from behind and sliding inside the defender's own penalty area are strongly discouraged.

FOUL FREQUENCY
- Challenges are now classified as ball-first or player-first at the contact frame.
- Clean ball-first tackles have a very low foul chance.
- Player-first, late, high-speed and from-behind challenges progressively increase foul risk.
- Removed the old automatic foul for every slide from behind.
- Removed the old rule that made an illegal collision an automatic foul when the victim was the user.
- Slide tackle contact is handled once by the tackle system, preventing duplicate collision foul calls.

FOUL PRESENTATION
- Added foul-specific reactions: stumble forward, trip forward, leg trip, swept leg, side sweep, body stumble, body knock and knockdown.
- Victim fall direction and severity are derived from the actual impact.
- The fouled player's reaction and tackler's slide are preserved through the initial referee stoppage presentation.
- The whistle/restart sequence now follows visible contact instead of visually appearing before any infringement.
- Foul metadata now records ball-first/player-first, animation variant and severity for debugging and referee presentation.

COMPATIBILITY
- Built on v44, retaining the true Penalty Training match system, true Free Kick Training match system, slower v42 match tempo, created-player stretcher rendering, and v38-v41 attribute gameplay systems.
