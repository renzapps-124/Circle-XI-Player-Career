# Circle XI Player Career — Match Gameplay Revamp V36

This build upgrades match fouls, aerial ball physics, crossing, directional locomotion, throw-ins, corners, free kicks, physio staff and defending controls.

## Defending controls
- I: pressure the opponent in possession
- J: call the nearest teammate to pressure
- K: block
- L: slide tackle

## Set pieces
- Match free kicks use the Free Kick Training technique family: Curl, Topspin Dip, Knuckle, Low Driven and Normal.
- Match corners use the Corner Training delivery family: Inswinger, Outswinger, Driven, Floated and Near-Post Whip.
- AI corners are deliveries into attacking zones rather than direct corner shots.

## Match physics
Lifted passes, crosses, chips, corners and free kicks use the shared x/y/z ball model with gravity, spin, drag, dip and bounce. Power/hold duration affects the launch profile.
