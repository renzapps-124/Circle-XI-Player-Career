PERFORMANCE GYM — RACE LONG TRACK CAMERA V23

Implemented:
- Race progression now uses world-distance as the authoritative movement state.
- Camera follows the controlled player instead of following the race leader.
- 100m, 200m and 500m use progressively larger world windows while remaining visibly longer than one viewport.
- Moving world-space distance markers, start/finish lines and scrolling track texture make forward movement visible while the camera tracks the runner.
- Longer races show distance remaining in the live HUD.
- Removed live dependence on fixed viewport start/finish overlays.
- Race timer now advances from the simulation timestep and freezes exactly at the user's finish time.
- Added per-runner progress sanitisation and a race movement watchdog to recover from stalled movement states.
- User and AI continue decelerating beyond the line while the result time remains frozen.
- AI runners continue to complete their race after the user crosses the line; remaining runners are only projected after a safety timeout.
- Camera remains stable through finish/deceleration.
- Reworked Race sprint biomechanics: stronger drive-phase lean, clearer front knee drive, larger rear-leg extension, shorter high-speed ground contact, more visible flight, speed-dependent stride length, opposite arm/leg coordination and fatigue-aware stride reduction.
- Match Day identity/rig remains the source model for the user and all team-mates.

Race attribute logic retained:
- Pace = maximum speed
- Acceleration = speed build-up
- Stamina = long-race retention
- J/L rhythm = efficiency, never an attribute override
- A+ remains maximum training grade
