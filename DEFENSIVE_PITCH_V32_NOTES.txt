CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — DEFENSIVE PITCH V32

Defensive Pitch has been rebuilt around three dedicated football-defending drills.

INTERCEPTION RONDO
- Uses recent shared Match Day player models for the user and squad teammates.
- Six possession players circulate the ball around the outside of a marked rondo grid.
- AI evaluates the user's position and passing-lane risk before choosing a receiver.
- User wins the ball by physically stepping across the live pass path.
- Interceptions and passing-lane closures score points; quicker reads earn a larger interception reward.
- Match-style pass, movement, first-touch and interception animations are used.
- Development profile: Agility, Positioning, Anticipation, Work Rate.

1V1 DEFENDING
- One AI teammate attacks an empty goal while the user starts goal-side.
- Attacker reacts to user positioning with changes of direction and acceleration rather than using one fixed path.
- K = standing tackle. L = slide tackle. I = jockey.
- Standing tackle has shorter reach and quicker recovery; slide has greater reach and greater risk.
- A missed tackle does not freeze the attacker: the attacker continues toward goal and can score into the empty net.
- Clean tackles earn points; missed tackles and goals conceded reduce the score.
- Development profile: Anticipation, Tackling, Positioning, Agility.

3V3 INTERCEPTION RONDO
- Replaces Recovery Run in the visible training list. Legacy Recovery Run selections migrate to this drill.
- Three possession players operate around the outside of the grid.
- User plus two AI defenders work as a defensive trio.
- Dynamic Press / Cover / Balance responsibilities are recalculated as the ball moves.
- AI defenders shift together and cover passing angles instead of all chasing the ball.
- Winning the ball enters a transition phase rather than resetting immediately.
- Defensive trio must secure two passes or the user can dribble through an EXIT.
- Players physically move into their new roles during the role switch.
- The former possession team then presses inside while the user's team keeps the ball outside.
- Development profile: Teamwork, Anticipation, Decisions, Agility.

SHARED DEFENSIVE PITCH UPGRADES
- New drill-specific pitch layouts and compact live HUDs.
- Recent Match Day body/appearance model system used instead of old generic training mannequins.
- Jockey movement uses the actual Match Day jockey animation state.
- Match-style passing, tackling, sliding, stretching and movement poses.
- Live score, phase, interception/tackle information and appropriate attribute chips.
- Ball remains physically in flight during passes/tackles/interceptions instead of teleporting between players.
- A+ remains the maximum training grade.

RUNTIME VALIDATION
- Interception Rondo: live AI passing, user interception and lane scoring tested.
- 1V1 Defending: clean standing tackle and empty-goal consequence tested.
- 3V3: live interception -> secure possession -> exit -> physical role switch -> possession phase tested.
- game.js passes Node JavaScript syntax validation.
