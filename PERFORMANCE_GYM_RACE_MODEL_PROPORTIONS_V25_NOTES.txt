PERFORMANCE GYM — RACE MODEL PROPORTIONS V25

Implemented after reviewing the V24 race-model screenshots.

Changes:
- Race-only thigh length reduced by 13% and shin length by 16% so legs no longer dominate the body.
- Hip height recalibrated for the shorter leg chain to retain an athletic sprint stance.
- Maximum race stride and knee lift reduced to prevent over-folded/crouched poses.
- Two-bone leg solver now penalises backwards knee solutions, over-high knees, and over-low knee positions.
- Front-knee guide moved forward/down into a more realistic sprint drive path.
- Rear-leg guide keeps the knee behind the hip during extension instead of folding underneath the body.
- Upper-arm roots separated farther from the torso for side-view readability.
- Upper arms are thicker and now include a visible sleeve/cuff marker so shoulder-to-elbow motion is readable.
- Forearms remain linked to the same fixed Match Day arm chain.
- Race trainer length increased by about 25%, width increased, while preserving side-profile heel/sole/toe shape.
- No Match Day identity data was changed; the Race mode still reads the canonical career/squad appearance.
- Race camera, 100m/200m/500m logic, Pace/Acceleration physics, J/L cadence, records and watchdog are unchanged.

Validation targets:
- No inverted knee solution.
- Fixed thigh/shin/arm lengths in every frame.
- Clearly visible upper arm on near-side runner.
- Larger trainers without returning to the oversized V21/V22 shoe look.
- Match Day identity fingerprint remains unchanged.
