SET-PIECE GOALKEEPERS STAY IN GOAL - V48 NOTES
==============================================

Requirement
-----------
A goalkeeper must not leave his goal to join a free-kick taker. At any dead ball, a keeper who
is not taking the restart himself belongs on his own line.

Root cause
----------
arrangeRestart() opens by dragging every player 68% of the way back to their formation slot,
then each restart type positions the players it cares about. Goalkeepers fell through that:

  - Only SOME branches placed a keeper. The free-kick branch handled both keepers correctly,
    which is why an ordinary free kick always looked right.
  - Where no branch placed him, a keeper caught upfield was left at 32% of the way out - out
    in the pitch, near the ball, for the whole set piece.
  - Two branches actively dragged a keeper towards the ball (see 2 and 3 below).

Reproduced with the keeper parked at (430,300) - roughly the centre circle - when the restart
was awarded. Distances below are from the centre of his own goal.

1. OFFSIDE INDIRECT FREE KICK - the reported case.
   The offside branch walks players to targets set at 12-18% back towards home, which barely
   moves anyone. The keeper's target was ~410px from his goal and 42px from the ball: he stood
   next to the free-kick taker for the whole restart. This is also the restart where it happens
   most, because a keeper is off his line exactly when his defence is playing the high line
   that produced the offside.

2. PENALTY - the taking side's keeper joined the queue on the edge of the box.
   The branch placed "every player except the taker and the defending keeper" in rows behind
   the penalty spot. That filter caught the attacking keeper, teleporting him from his own goal
   to ~770px away, into the ruck at the edge of the area.

3. GOAL KICK - the opposing keeper was pulled into midfield.
   "Every player on the other team" was spread across x = 310..450 from the kicking team's
   goal, which for the far keeper meant abandoning his line by ~700px.

4. THROW-IN, CORNER, KICK-OFF - keeper left at 173px from his goal, i.e. the 68% lerp and
   nothing else.

Fixes (game.js, all in arrangeRestart / updateRestartMovement)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- New keeper pass immediately after the generic lerp: any keeper who is not the taker is put
  on his own line (30px out, tracking the ball's y within the goal mouth). It runs BEFORE the
  type branches, so penalty / corner / free-kick keeper placements still override it with
  their more precise stances.
- Penalty branch: the crowd placement now excludes goalkeepers.
- Goal-kick branch: the opposition placement now excludes goalkeepers.
- Offside branch: after its own two passes, keeper restart targets are re-pointed at their own
  line, since those passes would otherwise overwrite the default.
- updateRestartMovement(): outfielders only shuffle a few metres, so snapping them at the end
  of the setup phase is invisible. A recovering keeper can be half a pitch out, so he keeps
  running past the setup phase, at a sprint (235 vs 125), instead of being teleported home.
  Measured maximum movement in a single frame during the recovery run: 3.9px.
- The unreachable else-branch fallback was made consistent (keepers excluded) so it cannot
  reintroduce the same bug if a new restart type ever lands there.

Verification
------------
Driven through the real engine (index.html?demo=tackle-fix-v45), both keepers parked at
(430,300) before the restart, measured once the restart reaches its 'ready' phase.
Distance from the centre of the keeper's own goal - the line itself sits at 30px, and anything
up to ~76px is the keeper shading across his goal mouth towards the ball:

  restart      taking side's GK    other GK      (before the fix)
  offside            73px             76px       ~410px / ~440px
  free kick          34px             29px       (already correct)
  penalty            30px             27px       ~770px  (edge of the box)
  goal kick     43px (he takes it)    30px       ~700px
  corner             70px             32px       173px
  throw-in           70px             70px       173px
  kick-off           30px             30px       173px

Post-goal kick-off (V48 change before this one) re-checked and unaffected: kick-off to the
conceding side, ball on the centre spot, nobody in the wrong half, both keepers on their lines.

Note on a NaN seen during testing: running dozens of scenarios inside one page session
eventually corrupted player coordinates in the test harness. It does not reproduce on a fresh
load - 30s of open play and a full corner sequence are clean - and it is unrelated to these
changes.

Not touched
-----------
preview.html, preview2.html, preview_head.html, _runtime_penalty_demo.html and
__inline_test.html carry their own inlined copies of the old engine.
