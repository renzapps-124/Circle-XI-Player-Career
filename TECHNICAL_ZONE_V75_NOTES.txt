CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TECHNICAL ZONE OVERHAUL v75
Base: v74 Finishing Pitch Overhaul

Implemented four live Technical Zone drills using the shared player/ball renderer and career attributes.

1) CLOSE CONTROL COURSE
- Technical Zone drill: Dribbling · Agility · Balance.
- Three rotating course layouts: slalom + bursts, figure-8 + gates, quick-feet + turns.
- WASD movement, M close-control modifier, Shift acceleration bursts.
- Close-control quality uses 45% Dribbling + 30% Agility + 25% Balance.
- Touch distance expands with sprint speed and sharp turns, and contracts with M.
- Cones/poles can be physically clipped; ball contact deflects the ball and slows the player.
- Cone touch, ball hit and heavy touch penalties are tracked.
- Checkpoints prevent one mistake from restarting the whole course.
- Losing control restarts from the latest checkpoint.
- Difficulty modifies control assistance and gate tolerance.

2) FIRST TOUCH ESCAPE
- Technical Zone drill: First Touch · Composure · Dribbling.
- Six incoming service variations: ground, driven, diagonal, lofted, bouncing and pass-behind.
- AI feeder and live pressing defender.
- First-touch quality uses 50% First Touch + 25% Composure + 25% Dribbling.
- User movement direction before reception determines the escape touch.
- The defender reacts to the actual ball position rather than a hidden target.
- A directional safe zone is generated away from pressure.
- User must carry the ball into the safe zone to complete the escape.
- Difficulty changes defender pressure and escape-space size.

3) PROTECT THE BALL
- Technical Zone drill: Strength · Balance · First Touch.
- SPACE enters the shielding state; WASD changes body position and turns.
- Shield quality uses 45% Strength + 35% Balance + 20% First Touch.
- Defender pressure is resolved as a progressive physical contest instead of instant possession loss.
- Ball-side/body orientation changes shielding effectiveness.
- Standing still increases defender leverage, preventing passive shielding exploits.
- Two scenario types rotate: Hold-Up Play and Shield → Turn.
- Turn variation requires escaping through a live gate after resisting contact.
- Difficulty changes contact pressure and available escape space.

4) WEAK FOOT DEVELOPMENT
- Moved into the Technical Zone.
- 60-second circuit cycles: Passing → Crossing → Finishing.
- Strong foot is locked for technical actions.
- J = weak-foot pass, I = weak-foot cross, L = weak-foot finish.
- Actual weak-foot rating modifies execution instead of replacing Passing/Crossing/Finishing/Technique/Composure.
- Existing weak-foot career progression is preserved, including weakFootProgress, star-rating growth and eventual both-footed development.
- Live UI shows weak-foot star rating and progression percentage.

SHARED TECHNICAL ZONE SYSTEM
- One Technical Zone runtime routes all four drills through shared movement, ball, renderer, scoring and result systems.
- 60-second live sessions.
- Dedicated compact Technical Zone HUD with objective, live status, score, streak, penalty and contextual controls.
- Drill-specific result metrics added for Close Control, First Touch Escape, Protect the Ball and Weak Foot Development.
- Existing career appearance/player renderer is used.
- v74 Finishing Pitch systems remain present.
- Browser cache tokens bumped to v75.0.0.
