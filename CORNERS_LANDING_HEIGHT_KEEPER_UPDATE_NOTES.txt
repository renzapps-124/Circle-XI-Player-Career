CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER – CORNER TRAINING LANDING / HEIGHT / KEEPER UPDATE

- Floated corners arrive higher and stay airborne longer.
- Driven corners remain lower and faster for volley-type chances.
- Near-post whip remains quick but still arrives at realistic aerial height.
