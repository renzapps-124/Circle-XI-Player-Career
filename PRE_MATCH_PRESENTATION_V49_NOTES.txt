Circle XI Player Career - Pre-Match Presentation V49

V49.1 Handshake Animation Upgrade
- Every home player is paired with the corresponding away player using one shared contact point.
- Greetings now run through approach, turn, reach, contact, shake, release, and move-on phases.
- Pairs animate in a short wave down the lineup instead of moving simultaneously.
- Both players face and look toward one another; early arrivals hold their approach until their shared phase begins.
- Eased hand reach/release, synchronized shake cycles, wrist rotation, shoulder lean, head nod, and forward-leg weight shift improve body language.
- Deterministic timing variation keeps the line natural while created-player and central featured greetings last slightly longer.
- Skip and state-transition cleanup removes every handshake pose before jogging to formation or enabling gameplay.

V49.2 Formation Preview GUI Upgrade
- Rebuilt the formation comparison as two larger broadcast cards with club-colour headers, larger crests, kit swatches, Home/Away labels, manager identity, and prominent formation badges.
- Added a responsive side-by-side layout for wide screens and stacked formation cards for narrow screens.
- Home and away teams reveal sequentially; each XI arrives by unit in goalkeeper, defence, midfield, attacking-midfield/wing, and striker order.
- Player markers now show shirt number, position, surname, goalkeeper styling, captain support, condition warnings, and a stronger pulsing YOU marker.
- Miniature pitches now include mowing stripes, grass depth, team-colour glow, complete markings, and a direction-of-attack indicator.
- Actual match tactical profiles supply playing style, press, tempo, and manager information.
- Formation-derived matchup notes compare midfield numbers, defensive lines, attacking numbers, and width using the real shared formation slots.
- The user matchup note explains the created player's actual match role.
- The role spotlight now renders the real created-player model and shows shirt number, match position, overall, preferred foot, natural role, selection status, manager instruction, and role brief.
- Anonymous stage dots were replaced by a labelled Matchday, Formations, Your Role, Walkout, and Kickoff progress line.
- Cards slide outward at the end of the state so the stadium/tunnel presentation becomes visible beneath them.

Implemented
- Nine-state pre-match controller: stadium, formations, user role, tunnel, walkout, lineup, handshakes, jog to positions, and kickoff readiness.
- Actual starting XIs, team colours, formations, player names, shirt numbers, ratings, and the created player's selected match position.
- A shared formation library used by presentation markers, kickoff positions, and tactical AI targets.
- Eleven supported formations: 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 4-4-2, 4-1-4-1, 4-3-2-1, 4-1-2-1-2, 3-4-3, 3-5-2, 5-4-1, 5-3-2, and 5-2-3.
- Existing match player instances are moved through tunnel, walkout, lineup, handshake, and formation paths. Created-player appearance and kit data are preserved.
- Referee and assistant referees lead the procession; the referee carries the match ball.
- Movement is continuous while visible. Walkout uses waypoints and jog-to-shape uses speed-limited movement.
- Player controls and match AI remain locked until the state machine completes and setupKickoff performs final cleanup.
- SPACE skips one phase. A second quick press, held SPACE, ENTER, or ESC skips safely to kickoff.
- Match Presentation setting: Full, Short, or Off. Full remains the default.

Preserved
- Existing half-time, full-time, trophy, replay, substitution, medical, dismissal, set-piece, AI, formation-change, and gameplay systems.
