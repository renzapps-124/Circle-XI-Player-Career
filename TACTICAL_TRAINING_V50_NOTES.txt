CIRCLE XI PLAYER CAREER — TACTICAL TRAINING v50.1
=================================================

ONE-MINUTE FORMAT

- Every advanced tactical drill now lasts exactly 60 seconds.
- The opening three-second countdown does not consume session time.
- Standard, Score Attack and Practice are all one-minute sessions.
- The timer changes to amber for the final 15 seconds and red for the final five.

RECEIVING AND PASSING FIX

- J, K or I can be pressed while the ball is travelling to the user.
- The selected pass is visibly marked as QUEUED.
- It executes immediately after the first touch establishes user possession.
- Passing, through-ball, crossing and shooting controls also work normally once
  the possession indicator reads YOUR POSSESSION.
- Aim-based teammate selection and visible passing lanes clarify the target.
- In All Round Circuit, the pass phase now locks J/K/I to the highlighted receiver.
- A possession safeguard restores user ownership if the first-touch state and
  ball-owner state briefly become unsynchronised before the pass input.
- The live coach explicitly displays PASS READY · J after the first touch.

PERMANENT BALL VISIBILITY

- The football now has explicit staged, owned, released, loose and recovered states.
- It remains visible during countdowns and scenario resets.
- It is rendered at the user, teammate or goalkeeper when controlled.
- A bright tracking ring and off-pitch locator make the ball easier to follow.
- A recovery safeguard restores any ball that loses a valid possession owner.

GRADING

- A+ is now the highest possible tactical grade.
- One-minute thresholds: A+ 5,500; A 4,400; B 3,300; C 2,300; D 1,300; F below 1,300.
- Historical S and S+ tactical grades are migrated to A+ for consistent displays.

GUI AND UI OVERHAUL

- Larger unobstructed live pitch with the side panel removed during play.
- Broadcast header for drill, timer, score, grade, streak and multiplier.
- One-minute progress bar and final-seconds warning treatment.
- Context-sensitive live coach objective and next-action panel.
- Permanent possession status and pressure indicator.
- Controls visibly change between locked, queueable, ready and queued states.
- Rebuilt setup screen with session summary, A+ grade scale, modes and difficulty.
- Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.
- Reduced-motion accessibility support.
