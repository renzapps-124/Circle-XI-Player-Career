# Circle XI v77.1 — Sequential Player Creator

The player creator now uses strict sequential progression.

- Only Identity is available when a new career begins.
- Club unlocks only after Identity is valid and the player presses Next.
- Role unlocks only after Club is valid.
- Style unlocks only after Role is valid.
- Review unlocks only after Style is valid.
- Previously unlocked steps can be revisited.
- If an earlier step becomes invalid, later steps are locked until it is corrected.
- Next and Begin Career are disabled when the current step is incomplete.
- Inline validation explains what is missing.

Validation includes blank names, invalid DOB/nationality, missing club pathway, duplicate positions, duplicate strengths, a weakness that duplicates a strength, invalid shirt numbers, and incomplete final career settings.
